Tactual Explorations: A tactile interpretation of a museum object
Date added:  2014-02-18 16:22:58
Modified:        2014-02-18 16:24:29
Author / Creator Isil Onol
This presentation introduces and evaluates the Tactual Explorations exhibition, a project formed as part of Isil Onol’s current PhD research investigating the topic of haptic interactions with museum objects that is currently being undertaken at the University of Huddersfield. Type of project: Research-based public exhibition Tech spec: A tactile interpretation consisting of a haptic simulation that is supported with tactile artworks created by commissioned artists. Comments: Tactual Explorations is the title of a research-based inclusive public event that took place in Huddersield - UK between 30 September and 7 October 2006; with workshops, talks and a tactile exhibition that interpreted one museum object. At this tactile exhibition, the selected object, the Bronze Bust of Sophocles from the British Museum’s Greek and Roman Antiquities collection is represented tactually by ten commissioned artists, through one haptic simulation and 12 supporting artworks to enhance the physical information. The exhibition was a pilot study for potential future exhibitions that would interpret individual museum objects tactually.

    Isil Onol

    University of Huddersfield
