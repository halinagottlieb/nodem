Bringing the kids along: Creating a new Kon-Tiki exhibition for both parents and children
Date added:  2013-10-16 09:24:59
Modified:        2014-01-15 19:16:08
Author / Creator Reidar Solsvikhttp://www.kon-tiki.no
Author / Creator Holger Fangelhttp://www.qvisten.no
Author / Creator Cory Grierhttp://www.artworx.no
Following release of the motion picture "Kon-Tiki" in August 2012, the museum decided to upgrade its main display: the story of the Kon-Tiki raft sailing from Peru to Polynesia in 1947. We also wanted to involve children in the story, an aspect sorely missed at the museum. Fishes, sea creatures and tiny animals are found on almost every page in the best seller by Thor Heyerdahl on the Kon-Tiki voyage. Amongst these interesting characters we found a tiny adventurous crab, by the crew nicknamed "Johannes", who we choose to the main character of the story and communicate with the children. "Johannes" had to be give a form and we needed to create a personality and supporting characters, with reference to the true story. The extremely limited space available for this exhibition proved the main challenge. Independent displays for children or separate activity areas could not be developed. The resulting exhibition had to somehow connect different age-groups in one flat display. We decided on developing a story-line where the exhibition for children followed our main story, so that families could pass through the exhibition experience the same story at similar pace, with connecting points now and again as they proceeded through the exhibition. The impressive visual design of the Kon-Tiki movie led us to seek collaboration with designers involved with the film project, choosing Artworx. A few years earlier Qvisten animation studio had developed a TV series based on the character of young Thor Heyerdahl, and we wanted their help in developing the children interface. The presentation tells the story about developing this new exhibition at the Kon-Tik Museum, and of some of the feedbacks from our visitors.
