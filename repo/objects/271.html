Touch of Kandinsky - How to make a carpet into an interactive or storytelling tool
Date added:  2014-01-07 21:30:37
Modified:        2014-01-07 21:45:05
Author / Creator Halina Gottliebhttp://www.nodem.org
Author / Creator David Nilsson
Touch of Kandinsky, an interactive tool in the form of a carpet with the motif of a painting, can be a playful complement to art galleries, which often challenge visitors’ perceptions. By encouraging visitors to interact with the motif on the carpet, the museum can enrich the experience of abstract art for both grown-ups and children alike. The carpet can be an important addition to the arranged curator-led activities in the gallery, offering an area for self-directed exploration. Attracting the visitor’s attention to the other paintings, the carpet can stimulate communication between visitors, sharing the experience of their simultaneous exploration. Authors: Halina Gottlieb, David Nilsson
