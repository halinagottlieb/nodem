Touch and Turn
Date added:  2013-12-31 12:31:08
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:14:23
Museum/site: T&T Virtual Library Country: Sweden Developers: Touch & Turn AB in collaboration with Raketspel Interaktiva, and 8 partners in 5 different countries:The project partners are: Karl-Franzens University Library in Graz, Austria Zentral und Landesbibliothek Berlin, Germany Swedish Institute at Athens, Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: eTEN A Community Programme to help the roll-out of e-services across Europe, http://europa.eu.int/eten Project website: http://www.touchandturn.com Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2003 End year: 2004 Summary: The T&T Virtual Library is the result of an unique collaboration between governemental institutions and private companies within the project "Mediation of rare books over the Internet". The purpose is to develop and validate an online platform where digitial facsimiles of rare books and manuscripts can for the first time be completly accessible to educationals, students and the general public with the T&T technology. The platform is also intented to become the community for rarebook holders in Europe, using a technology that can serve as a standard for presenting unique material. Read more att www.touchandturn.com/eTEN.htm Intentions Rare books and documents ar unque, fragile, valuable, difficult to read, detailed and therefor not accessible to the general public. By using the T&T technology through the Internet the user will for the first time be able to access unique and valuable rarities like the Icelandic Edda, 14 century Codex and first editions of books that are not avaliable to the public, in the most realistic way at home or work. The navigation and design is developed with a pedagogical aim to put the original work in fokus, making it even more accesible by using funcitons for -translation ( ut to three languages at once) enlargments facts and comments text browsing page navigator The user is thus encouraged to both experience the object as such and to get knowledge about the content. The research aspect is very important, making requested books avaliable in a fast and easy way for researchers all around the world. The Internet Library is at the same time a meeting area for everyone that has an interest in rare books and manuscript encouraging a dialogue between the rarebook holders and their end users. While the cultural heritage gets more accesible and the original is safely preserved. Tech spec: The T&T technology consists of a software to publish rare books (Publisher) and an integrated interface (Viewer)for virtual browsing. The Publisher has made it possible for the project partners to create interactive books from their collections and to make them accesssible for exhibitions, education, research or private use. Stability and performance is ensured by working with standard formats for text and images. THe technology is developed with Macromedia Director. At the T&T Virtual Library the T&T technology is developed even further, which will be demonstraded in the new T&T Viewer 2.0, launched in March 2004. The Viewer will have a new navigation and magnifying function, demonstrated in some of the demos in the library collection. The user will for the first time be able to search the complete text in the translation and comment functions. To download the Viewer you will have to become a Library member. Then you can download fiels of online versions of books that you are interested to explore. These online versions are reduced in size and adapted to the capacity of the user. High-quality versions of each item will be avaliable in a CD Rom Virtual Library Edition and possible to purchase in the T&T Webshop. Comments: The website was launched on the 26th of January 2004 and the mediation service will be completed in March 2004. This should be taken under consideration. Today we are only presenting demos of the books as a preview.
