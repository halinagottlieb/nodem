Museum Finland - Finnish Museums on the Semantic Web
Date added:  2013-12-31 11:13:50
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:36:59
Museum/site: MuseumFinland Country: Finland Developers: E. Hyvönen (director), M. Junnila, S. Kettula, E. Mäkelä, S. Saarela, M. Salminen, A. Syreeni, A. Valo, K. Viljanen Sponsor partnerships: National Museum, Espoo City Museum, Lahti City Museum, Antikvaria Group consisting of some 20 Finnish museums, Foundation of the Helsinki University Museum, Nokia Corp TietoEnator Corp, National Technology Agency Tekes Submittor Helsinki Institute for Information Technology (HIIT), University of Helsinki Project website: Research group: http://www.cs.helsinki.fi/group/seco/, Project (in Finnish): http://www.cs.helsinki.fi/group/seco/museosuomi/ Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2002 End year: 2004 Summary: The project has created a pilot version of a new kind of web publication channel and a semantic portal for the Finnish museums. By the new semantic web technology, collections from different museums and of different kind can be made interoperable in content, and we can provide the audience with new kind of intelligent services for searching and browsing the global Finnish cultural heritage. Intentions: Museum Finland is intended for the public in the large. User experience is illustrated on the in included MW2004 conference presentation (in English) and in the include additional overview article in Finnish. Tech spec: A 2-page system summary is included as a separate document. Comments: The attached paper to be presented at the Museums and the Web 2004, USA, conference illustrates the system from the user's perspective. According to the plan, the portal will be opened on the March 8, 2004, at the National Museum.
