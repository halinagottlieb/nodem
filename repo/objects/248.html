The Avesta Works / Verket
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:10:18
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:16:26
Museum/site: Verket Country: Sweden Developers: Avesta kommun, Kultur Ineractive Institute, Smart studio Snap AB Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Kultur Avesta, Interactive Institute, Smart studio Project website: http://tii.se/avesta/ Project status: completed Project type: Large scale/Immersive Installation Start year: 2001 End year: 2004 Summary: The historical environment with the blast furnace is, in itself, worth a visit but there are more possible ways of attracting visitors. By bringing new ideas into the site, Avesta Iron Works can become even more interesting and a place worth returning to. The installations that are now being developed in the blast furnace building together with Interactive Institute and in the site of the open-hearth process together with Snap AB, will enhance and deepen the experience of the area. Filling the environment with pedagogical, Interactive, cultural and experimental material will do this. It will go beyond contemporary exhibition and stage design and information booths. The planned interactive technique will be inserted into the environment, giving the visitor the experience of a dark and mysterious place. The project’s ambition is to enrich the cultural heritage in the old blast furnace building with an interactive layer, thereby creating a learning and sensual environment; a meeting place for history, science and art in a new and inspiring way. In Avesta’s old iron works there are two well-preserved furnaces of the open-hearth process. In cooperation with SNAP one of the furnaces will gestalt the “Open-Hearth Furnace Opera”. The title for this project is “Verket” (the Works). Intentions: The visitors to “Verket” are expected to have a wide range of different backgrounds in terms of age, sex and education. It is for this reason that “Verket” is a platform that will easily meet various needs. At present, there are currently two levels of content – the pedagogical level and the poetic level. The pedagogical level supplies facts about the production process but also makes use of poetical effects. The poetical level describes, in an artistic and sensuous way, daily life and work, thoughts and ambitions that took place in this extreme environment. Tech spec Imagine you receive a tool to explore and animate this environment. In Verket this tool is a special flashlight. Each flash light has a specific way of releasing information and effects in the environment; e.g.: the educational flashlight is releasing explanations of the steel production process, while the poetic flashlight is making the environment respond with personal stories and evocative effects. The keyword is personalization of the experience. To trigger those effects, the space is equipped with special hyperspots that that can pick up a coded and invisible beam coming from the flashlights. The hyperspots mark enabled zones of interest to the visitor, and they can be found throughout the blast furnace hall, for example the tapping of the blast furnace and its various details, When the visitor points the flashlight at a point of interest, or hyperspot, the environment can respond with a variety of audio-visual media, spotlights, projections or even physical effects. The experience content is stretching invisibly through the area of the plant, forming topical layers of media, each of which are associated with one specific topical flashlight. The flashlight becomes the navigational instrument. Comments: Verket will open in the middle of May 2004. Homepage Verket .se will open in march.
