Museum Learning and Gidder
Date added:  2014-02-20 14:51:27
Modified:        2014-12-09 09:50:24
Author / Creator Palmyre Pierrouxhttp://www.uv.uio.no/iped/engl...
Mobile technologies designed for use in schools and museums often have specific and fundamentally pedagogical objectives. At the same time, we know that people too are mobile and that participation in all kinds of activity, mediated by a rich complexity of semiotic resources, are essential to learning. This is the analytical challenge I will be exploring in the mobile design research project Gidder. In this presentation I describe this project and the theoretical perspective that informs its design. Institution: InterMedia, University of Oslo Type of project: Learning about art with wiki and mobile phone technologies across classroom and museum settings. Comments: Project starts Jan. 2007. Digital Design research program, InterMedia, University of Oslo.

    Palmyre Pierroux

    Palmyre Pierroux is Associate Professor at the Department of Education, University of Oslo, where she leads the InterMedia research group. Pierroux received her PhD in educational psychology and has a background in art history, design, and architecture. Pierroux’s research focuses on meaning making in museums and other semi-formal learning contexts, and on the role of new media and technologies in different aspects of knowledge production. Pierroux leads the nationally funded CONTACT project (2009-2013), which explores the design and use social media and digital technologies for meaning making in museum settings.
