Asia Art Archive

Author / Creator Chantal Wong
Many archives today are rethinking the conventional methods of the archive, acting as generators of new paradigms and practices. An archive’s collection is the foundation of its day-to-day practices as well as the nucleus of its potential to generate new methodologies in research, knowledge production, and artistic and curatorial activity. Looking at the strategies and methods of some of the most forward thinking archives on art, this lecture will reflect on a growing interest in the subject of archives around the world and their responses to the political and historical legacies and frameworks out of which they have emerged. More than a static repository waiting to be discovered, Asia Art Archive is a proactive platform, its collection existing not in an enclosed space, but in a space that is open and productive, generating new ideas and works that continually reshape the Archive itself. Taking Asia Art Archive as a starting point, this study will explore key concerns of leading art archives today — accessibility and usership, political activation, interpretation, collectivity, connectivity and discursive practices — and their hopes to re-dress paradigms of visibility through the activation of their content.

Chantal Wong

Chantal Wong holds degrees in Art History and Cultural Studies. She has worked in development at Asia Art Archive, HK and Outset Contemporary Art Fund, London and is currently the Head of Strategy and Special Projects at Asia Art Archive.
