Culture Greenland
Date added:  2013-12-31 10:48:24
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:38:07
Museum/site: Sisimiut Museum Country: Greenland Developers: Klaus Georg Hansen (project manager), Michael Husen, Jakub Medonos, Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Sisimiut Museum Project website: http://www.culture.gl Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2001 End year: 2000 Summary: The portal Culture Greenland was launched in 2001. The aim is to be the most comprehensive and most updated portal in Greenland regarding culture related websites and information. Within the frames of the Culture Greenland site two databases have been created up till now. The image database was launched in February 2002. It now contains more then 8.500 images. The biography database is about to be launched in the beginning of March 2004. It is the intention to extend Culture Greenland with a GIS-based map of Greenland and other databases. A prototype of the map was created in 2003 in cooperation with Technical University of Denmark. Likewise we have a prototype of a sound database. But both these projects are awaiting external funding to ensure they can be carried through. Intentions We have aimed at creating possibilities for the visitors to look up information, to provide new information, and to individually organize the information in the databases.
