Photos for everybody – experiencies from an on line collection web page as an instrument for audience work
Date added:  2013-10-16 08:57:55
Modified:        2013-10-16 08:57:55
Author / Creator Erja Salohttp://www.valokuvataiteenmuse...
”Photos for everybody” is a collection webpage. It includes nearly 200 photos which are free for visitors to use and share for example in facebook or in pinterest. The page was launched in November 2013 and the starting point to its content was the meeting with geriatric nurses with whom The Finnish Museum of Photography had worked together previously in The Photography Refreshes –project. After this succesful project where photography helped to build bridges between observations, memory and old people's own personal experiences, the nurses hoped for material which could be used independently and free in the internet. That is how we then built the “Photos for everybody” page where focus now is on the 1950´s and 60´s documentary photography around such topics as Helsinki, Politicians, and Every day life - since those were the fruitful themes which proved useful in the elderly peoples homes. Later this autumn and in the beginning of the year 2014 the page will be used with totally different group. The project is called ”The Finnish Museum of Photography goes Kontula” and it is a participatory reach-out project where a group of Kontula suburb community art activists curate a row of three exhibitions (nov 2013-aug 2014)to their local gallery called Kontupiste. The basis for their curatorial work are the photoworks shown in the ”Photos for everybody” netpage but the project will put them in a totally new context in the form of rich public program, text producing workshop etc.

    Erja Salo

    Head of Education and Public programmes / The Finnish Museum of Photography
