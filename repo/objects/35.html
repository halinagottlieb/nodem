Europeana and the Future of the Archive
Date added:  2013-09-17 21:19:03
Modified:        2013-09-19 12:44:51
Author / Creator Harry Verwayen
The Europeana Foundation was set up in 2008 for the purpose of fostering collaboration between the Museums, Libraries, Archives and Audiovisual Collections of Europe. This collaboration has resulted in a strong network of over 2200 partners from 33 countries, working together to make this collective heritage accessible in meaningful new ways. The results of this collaboration is a repository of over 24 million objects, which will be released under the Public Domain Mark (CC0) in September 2012. We believe that there is strong potential for re-usable digital culture to improve our educational systems, build new innovative businesses and to generally enrich lives. But making this a reality will require different kinds of partnerships, with the creative industry, educational publishers and designers. During this talk we will explore these new challenges by looking closely at a number of recent projects, including Open Labs, First World War crowdsourcing and ECloud, a 3D installation that was created in partnership with the Applied Laboratory for Interactive Visualization and Embodiment (ALiVE), CityU of Hong Kong.

    Harry Verwayen

    Harry Verwayen - Deputy Director Europeana. I am responsible for the strategy, business and product development of Europeana , Europe’s platform for digital cultural heritage. My main passion is the design and implementation of new models that will support us in our aim to make our complete heritage openly accessible for work, learning and pleasure. Prior to this I worked at the Amsterdam based thinktank Knowledgeland http://www.knowledgeland.org> where I was responsible for business model innovation and the Images for the Future project. I studied History at Leiden University and worked over ten years in the Scientific Publishing Industry. Mediocre football player, reasonable cook, aspiring photographer.
