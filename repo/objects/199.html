Combining digital storytelling with user experience design
Date added:  2013-10-21 10:22:18
Modified:        2013-10-23 00:04:48
Author / Creator Stefan Göbel
Based on the Inspiration Session 2, various methods, integrated concepts and application examples for Storytelling based Edutainment applications are discussed. This include technical issues such as authoring tools, story models, story engines, run-time systems or interaction meta-phors as well as general issues and practical questions: Who is involved? How long does it take to "produce" a story? Which learning models are integrated? Which technical requirement (and costs) is necessary for museums? All these questions are discussed in the context of existing applications and projects, e.g. DinoHunter applications for the Senckenberg Museum in Frankfurt, Germany.

    Stefan Göbel

    Zentrum für Graphische Datenverarbeitung, Germany
