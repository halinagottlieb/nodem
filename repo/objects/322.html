A common digital database of the local history archives. It will be accessible on the Internet through a wiki-application, where students from the loca schools and other visitors will be able to consult the database and add content to make it grow. Any kind of file can be added. Institution: Visions for Museums, Svenska kulturfonden i Finland, Finlands Svenska Hembygdsförbund Submitted by: Pargas hembygdsmuseum, Kilens hembygdsgård, Bjärgas hembygdsgård & museum Type of project: Web-based application Comments: Planned to be published in December 2006
