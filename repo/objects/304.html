Digital Experiences at the Hellisheidi Geothermal Power Plant
Date added:  2014-02-02 13:01:16
Modified:        2014-06-09 08:50:33
Organization/company: Orkuveita Reykajvikur (Reykjavik Energy), Gagarín and Nýherji websites: www.or.is http://www.or.is/flash/brunnur_or/index.html http://www.or.is/flash/framl/index.html A visiting centre has been opened at the geothermal plant on Hellisheidi heath where a multimedia exhibition has been installed. The exhibition illustrates through interactive media how energy is harnessed at the power plant. Visitors can experience the power of the natural forces and learn about the Hellisheiði area, its nature and cultural history. Gagarin took an active role in the design of the exhibition from the initialidea and throughout the process in cooperation with the exhibition designer Árni Páll Jóhannsson. The focus was to use the power plant itself, the building and the area around it as an important part of the "guests experience" and great emphasis was on making the show an inseparable part of the building. Gagarin produced the digital content and software solutions in the following installations: • The harnessing cycle of the geothermal power plant • The well (Brunnurinn) • Earth core (Jarðsúlan) • Earthquake sensors (Jarðskjálftamælar) • The geothermal system (Jarðhitakerfið) The hardware solutions are provided by Nyherji (Sense).
