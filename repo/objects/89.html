Tradition (re)visited: from place to presence
Date added:  2013-09-24 11:53:02
Modified:        2013-09-24 11:53:02
Author / Creator Chiel van den Akkerhttp://www.let.vu.nl/nl/organi...
Three ways of encountering objects belonging to our cultural heritage can be distinguished.We may encounter an object in its traditional environment, we may encounter an object in an environment of preservation, and we may encounter an object in an environment of representation. These three ways of encountering objects provide a framework for addressing the question how we relate ourselves to cultural heritage by means of digital media. Digital reproductions of our cultural heritage belong to the third way of encountering objects. In our present day we first and foremost relate ourselves to tradition by representing it, whereas before we first and foremost related ourselves to tradition by remembering it. Places of memory are exchanged for the presence of representation in a culture driven by information technology.
