The whispering carpet
Date added:  2014-02-18 16:31:18
Modified:        2014-02-18 16:31:18
Author / Creator Bente Ruud
A carpet can carry you away to faraway places and into ages long forgotten. It might even whisper a story about a hero and his son, lost love and destiny. Carpets are an obvious focus in an exhibition about the cultural diversity of Caucasus. The whispering carpet is a copy of an original shown in the exhibition with motives from Persian poetry of the 10th century. Through data technology it tells its story while designs of lights and sounds underline the changing moods. institution: Museum of Archaeology Stavanger, Interactive Institute Stockholm Submitted by: Bente Ruud, Siv Kristoffersen AmS, Halina Gottlieb, Lotte Hougaard, V4m Type of project: Experience room as part of an exhibition
