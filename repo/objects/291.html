Hallwyl House Online Exhibition
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:26:46
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:26:46
Author / Creator Sara Dixon
Organization/company: Hallwyl House Museum Presenter: Sara Dixon Type of project: Online web exhibition The Hallwyl House Museum was the home of antiques collector Wilhelmina von Hallwyl and her husband, the Swiss count Walther von Hallwyl. It was built between 1894 and 1898 and contained all of the latest technical inventions of its time. It had electrical lighting in all rooms, running hot and cold water, central heating and a water closet. The kitchen was the most obvious exponent for all this technology. The Hallwyl House was donated to the Swedish state and now forms an administrative authority together with two other museums, and as an authority we have strict requirements for ensuring accessibility. With the aim to increase accessibility at the same time as showing and explaining a seldom seen part of the museum, we have decided to make an online exhibition about the museum kitchen. The ambition is total accessibility for a number of defined target groups in combination with interesting functionality, a pedagogical approach and modern design communicating a turn of the century atmosphere.
