Virtual exhibition of Polyteekkarimuseo
Date added:  2013-12-31 12:38:27
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:40:18
Museum/site: Polyteekkarimuseo Country: Finland Developers: Committee that takes care of maintenance, development, planning and construction of exhibitions in Polyteekkarimuseo. Committee is comprised of students of Helsinki University of Technology and they do the work voluntarily. Layout and design of the web pages was made by Lauri Sumari. Sponsor partnerships: No actual sponsors. Museum is owned by Studen Union of Helsinki University of Tehchnology. Submitted by: Student Union of Helsinki University of Technology Project website: http://www.polyteekkarimuseo.fi Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2003 End year: 2004 Summary: October 2003: The idea of a new web site with a virtual museum arose and designing was started. November 2003: Rough version of the web site was released for testing purposes. February 2004: Bugs found from the test version will be fixed and the web site will be published in Finnish. March 2004: The web site will be translated to Swedish and English. April 2004: The web site will be ready and advertising begins locally on our science society. Intentions: The web site will provide access to our museum exhibition virtually anywhere anytime. It is a kind of supplementary service that our museum offers. Visitors, especially former students of Helsinki University of Technology, will be this way offered a possibility to see our current exhibition. This project will also help disabled people because the physical location of our museum is not easily accessible for them. Also real temporary exhibitions of our museum will be documented and archived for later use. Tech spec: The web site is valid HTML 4.01 and valid CSS. Interactive features are made with JavaScript. These techniques were chosen to provide platform free access to our web site from any web browser. Comments; First part of the project is already implemented. Further development is going on and the project is going to be finished in the end of April 2004.
