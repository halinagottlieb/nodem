The Castle PeepBox - A painting comes to life
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:29:38
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:29:38
Author / Creator David Nilsson
Author / Creator Hans Öjmyr
Organization/company: Evoking Spaces and Stockholm City Museum, Sweden Presenters: David Nilsson and Hans Öjmyr Type of project: Permanent installation, interpretive tool, audiovisual experience Website: http://evokingspaces.se/castle.html The Castle PeepBox is a permanent museum installation at the Stockholm City Museum which conveys a fun and novel experience of a 17th century oil painting of the old Swedish royal castle - Three Crowns. The aim of the installation is to raise interest of the original painting and the history of Stockholm with regards to this period of time. One side of the Castle PeepBox displays a replica of the painting and in the middle of the painting the visitor finds a small peephole. When visitors look through the peephole, a film sequence is triggered and is projected from the dark depths of the box. Specific scenes from the painting are zoomed in on and dramatized scenes are acted out on the street in front of the castle by characters in the painting. The sequence is accompanied by ambient sounds of the cityscape. If the visitor looks away, the sound and the illuminated image switch off. If one looks again, a new scene is zoomed in on and acted out. The Castle PeepBox originates from a prototype developed at the Interactive Institute in 2007. The aim of the prototype, called Electro Bacchanalia, was to stimulate young visitors' interest in the motif of the old master's painting by using modern technology and contemporary references. The result was a successful museum installation in the form of an interactive peep box, which brought a 17th century oil painting to life in a fun and novel way. The same method was used to create the Castle PeepBox, which is one of many unique peep boxes in the Interactive PeepBox series that is being produced and marketed by Evoking Spaces. Evoking Spaces is a spin off company from the Interactive Institute that designs audiovisual experiences with the aim to stimulate users curiosity, creativity and learning ability. Partners The Stockholm City Museum's function is to preserve the city's cultural heritage, bring it to life and convey it to Stockholm residents, visitors and future generations. The Stockholm City Museum was awarded "Best Museum in Stockholm 2007".
