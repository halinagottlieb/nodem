Curating assistance with the 4K/8K ultra high-resolution interactive virtual museum. Providing information and context
Date added:  2013-10-16 12:05:03
Modified:        2013-10-16 12:05:04
Author / Creator Steven Bachelderhttp://www.speldesign.uu.se
Author / Creator Masaki Hayashihttp://www.speldesign.uu.se
Author / Creator Masayuki Nakajimahttp://www.speldesign.uu.se
Museums and their artifacts offer rich and deep sources of information and experience with links to many other artifacts in and outside of specific collations. Much of this can only be accessed in direct contact with the artifacts themselves or via research facilities. The connection between the artifacts and the rich contexts from which they come is often severed rendering the artifacts displaced, fragmented or incomplete when displayed in museums. Much of the documentation of complex and precious artifacts is done with standard photography only. Our solution provides digital 3D, 4K/8K ultra high-resolution documentation and representation for local and distributed access of both 2D and 3D-artifacts. This provides ultra high-resolution digital artifacts in ultra high-resolution virtual environments for display on 4K and 8K screens and projectors. This is done by the use of high-resolution 3D graphics displayed in a game engine used for immersive 3D computer games. As the representation and delivery format is digital, links between objects and their contexts and histories can be curated either in virtual museums or collections that can be accessed directly on the internet or used for the purpose of education, research, or for planning and documentation of curatorial work.
