ARC 3D - How to turn images of an object or scene into 3D models for exhibitions and archives
Date added:  2014-01-07 21:38:25
Modified:        2014-01-07 21:38:35
Author / Creator David Nilsson
Author / Creator Daniel Pletinckx
Author / Creator Luc Van Gool
Author / Creator Maarten Vergauwen
The cultural heritage community has always had a keen interest in the creation of 3D models of objects, monuments, scenes, etc. Such models not only help in creating a more vivid picture of the past in the mind of the general public, but they are also of great value for training and research within the field. The ARC 3D Webservice enables its users to transform images of scenes or objects into 3D models that enhance the experience of both current and archived collections.
