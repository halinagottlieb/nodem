Face By Face
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:14:51
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:17:24
Museum/site: Vadstena Kulturcentrum, "Mitt (i) Vadstena" Country: Sweden Developers: Peter Blom, Kosta Economou, Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: NVIS - Norrköping Visualization and Interaction Studio Project website -- Project status: completed Project type: Large scale/Immersive Installation Start year: 2003 End year: 2003 Summary: 2003 saw the 700 year celebration of Swedish saint Bridget, with festivities centered around her hometown of Vadstena. Given a space within a city historical exhibition in connection with the festivities, we sought to give a view alternative to the established on sainthood and holiness. In the project, S:t Bridget is juxtaposed to Predikare-Lena (Preacher-Lena), who in the 19th century was commited to the Vadstena insane asylum - another large part of Vadstena. Intentions: The project enables each visitor/user to interact with the AR-mask, to experience the content and simultaneously be part of the installation for other spectators. The project had around 13.000 visitors during the months of june to august 2003. It was also represented at the Book- and Library fair in Gothenburg september 2003. Tech spec The project is created in an augmented reality (AR) format, integrated with exhibition design and audiovisual display within a framework of art installation. The display system (Linus) used was a prototype developed by Linus Display AB (www.linusdisplay.com). Linus enables a high quality video image to be generated by a small, light weight display unit that has the same width as the desired picture but its physical dimensions are only a few centimetres high and deep. The principle of Linus is that the many lines making up a video picture are put on just one line. The image of a full screen is then created by means of special glasses worn by each viewer. The glasses deflect the successive lines of the line display and restore the picture on the eye. Comments: At Nodem the project will be displayed in a simplified version, if space allows.
