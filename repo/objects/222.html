Museum/site: grotto Country: Nordic Developers: manuscript: Tomas Ivan Träskman design: White Sheep Productions: Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Nordic Institute for Contemporary Art (NIFCA) Project website: www.grotto.tv Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2003 End year: 2004 Summary: Grotto.tv was created in order to create dialogoue. A content that is hardly ever recognized when talking about content production. Dialogue is produced when somone is speaking to someone. Speaking means speaking to someone and thinking for someone. And this someone is always a quite definite someone, and he has not merely ears... but also a mouth... Speech is bound by time and nourished by time and it neither can nor wants to abandon this element. It does not know in advance just where it will end. It takes its cues from others. In fact, it lives by virtue of anothers life, whether this other is the one who listens to a story, answers in the course of a dialogue, or joins in a chorus... In actual conversations something happens. There is nothing solid in speech that would threaten the contemporary happiness of mobility. By contrast- something happens! Yet there is but speed and acceleration - no time. Does all this implicate that speech is no longer a valid form of communication? Sounds stupid, doesn't it. Grotto.tv operates both on the site and through workshops with schools, konferences and universities in order to investigate the contemporary problematics of media and medialization. Grotto.tv tries to create reflexion and analysis in a time that has no time. Intentions For Grotto TV to become a truly critical platform something else then just mediation is needed, this is done through Interactivity. But, interactivity is developed into something that is quite different to what normally is considered as interactive. Since the aim of Grotto.tv is to make people contemplate and talk about “the media” and medialization of the world. Where media are not viewed as a set of institutions, but a myriad of communicative forms or expressive techniques ranging from soaps to cyber worlds, the distinction between true/false is abandoned. Real life is becoming in words “indistinguishable from the movies”. The perspective changes when we see media/society as mutually inherent and mutually constitutive. Interactivity in this context means interactivity in the real world that cannot be separeated from the mediated one. This is done when other people get involved, engaged and develop Grotto.tv further, because now it is just a machinery or a platform, that can be empty as well as sparkling with energy. The work during the year 2004 will focus very much on gathering these “thinking creatures” and producing the energy needed. Tech spec: A site www.grotto.tv streams out the dialogues in Quick Time format. The streaming technology is a software called Optibase and which uses Mpeg4 code. Comments: www.grotto.tv lanseras i början på år 2004 på Reykjavik Art Museum.
