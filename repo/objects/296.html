Kongehøjen
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:38:47
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:38:47
Author / Creator Camilla Bjarnø
Organization/company: Moesgård Museum, Denmark Presenter: Camilla Bjarnø Type of project: Website, incl. online game Kongehøjen is a digital pioneer project primarily focused on 3D visualisation, supported by The Danish Ministry of Culture. The main feature of the website is a photogram metric model of Kongehøjen - a spectacular megalithic grave from Mariager Fjord in Denmark. Besides providing a realistic recording of the present state of the monument, the model also makes it possible to make a virtual visit. Hotspots on the model gives us the opportunity to highlight and explain details of the construction, which is not an option at the physical site, and also can be difficult to observe for the untrained eye. There by making it possible to exhibit monuments of a scale and nature, otherwise not suited for exhibiting in a museum. 3D-visualisations of finds from the site, gives you the opportunity to study the object in detail from all sides, and are the nearest, you can get to have the object in your hand. A special feature is the 3D online game (one player) where you step-by-step build a megalithic passage grave (Jordhøj), in a 3D reconstruction of the landscape 3200 B.C.
