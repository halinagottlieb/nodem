Organization/company: Finlands svenska hembygdsförbund, Finland Submitted by: Anne Bergström-Stenbäck Website: www.museilaboratoriet.fi The Museum Laboratory is an internet-based cultural heritage application. The website consists of exhibitions where archive and museum items from different Home district museums in the swedish-speaking area are presented. The website is a wiki-media based webapplication and it gives the public possibility to consult previously not available material and possibility to add their own material in different forms. Target groups are school groups, teachers and persons interested in their home district.
