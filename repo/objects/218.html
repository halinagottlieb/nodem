NODEM 2004 4-5 May 2004, Kiasma Museum of Contemporary Art Helsinki, Finland Nodem 04 took place in Helsinki, Finland, 4-5 May 2004. The program included presentations of nominated Nordic projects, inspiration sessions, exhibition, workshops and excursions to sites in Helsinki. This website contains presentation of Nodem 04 award winners, a total list of Nordic projects submitted for award nomination, as well as photos from the event.
