The museum as a gaming board
Date added:  2013-10-16 12:16:12
Modified:        2013-10-16 12:16:13
Author / Creator Arnfinn Stendahl Roknehttp://www.ntnu.no/vitenskapsm...
For the last three years the NTNU University Museum, Trondheim, Norway, has experimented with smart-phone games in the exhibitions. The museum has establish a cooperation with the Department of Computer and Information Science, NTNU where student groups toghether with museum staff develop games aimed at children and families. Using student groups has enabled the museum to test out ideas at a very low cost, and to learn more about the use of games in museum exhibitions. The procjets are very process focused and aims to be a place of learning and experimenting with digital media for museum staff. The games have been made with som main goals for the visitors; to create a social experience, to make people aware of object and displays in the exhibitions and create experiences that are unique to the museum. The genral idea is to use the museum as a gaming board, and technology as a tool to involve the audience in a story or as a vechicle for moving the game forward. The poster will present some of our project, the way we work with digital media and what we have gained from the project so far.v We would especially like to share the great benefits of working with student project.
