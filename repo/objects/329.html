Safety Nets, Security States, Curatoria Euphorias: Some Observations and Questions about Museum Cultures in the Early 21st Century
Date added:  2014-11-23 20:15:03
Modified:        2015-01-15 20:11:10
Author / Creator Mary Anne Staniszewski
Featured in the presentation will be an analysis of developments, transformations, and phenomena related to the proliferation of global networks, big data, surveillance, and algorithmic solutions during the past several years. Questions will be raised regarding how curatorial and museological practices are functioning as a means of offering a sense of security, neutrality, and control in an increasingly infiltrated and digitally dystopic social landscape. Examined will be the ways that the curatorial and the technological intersect: metaphorically, actually, and potentially in the post 9-11 environment. The history of terrorisms in the United States will be addressed with reference to mythologies and museums. There will be a focus on the National September 11 Memorial Museum, which opened this year in New York City – what is literally ground zero for much of the contemporary US discourse on these matters.
