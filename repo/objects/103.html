Museu do Resgate (Museum of Ransom): can everyday videos proclaim documental and patrimonial values?
Date added:  2013-10-14 21:46:59
Modified:        2013-12-30 14:53:45
Author / Creator Daniel Brandão
Author / Creator Heitor Alvelos
Author / Creator Nuno Duarte Martins
In this paper we will present the project “Museu do Resgate” (Museum of Ransom), which has been developed within the PhD Program in Digital Media in the University of Porto. Born in June 2012, this project’s mission is to gather on a website different views on everyday local cultures, recorded on video. The main objective is to contribute to the discussion, identified in an early stage of the investigation, about a new museum concept, which seems to be growing online, the “museum of all”. This is being built collectively and emerges from the proliferation of new tools for creating and sharing digital content online, that many use to document their daily lives. Our research project aims at contributing to the reading of the emerging concepts of “documentary”. We seek to understand how digital media can contribute, through its strongly ubiquitous, social and participatory characteristics, to the inclusion of citizens in documentary practice. Consequently, we seek to understand what can the documental and patrimonial value of everyday videos be. The practical project was launched with the support of the City Council’s cultural program “Manobras no Porto”, that during 2011 and 2012, aimed at encouraging and financing projects that wanted to explore their interactions with local communities in the Historical Center of Porto. Given the inclusive nature of our project and the importance of citizens’ natural and spontaneous participation, we adopted a set of diverse research methods with exploratory and ethnographic characteristics. Therefore, in this paper, we will expose the methodological approach as well as reveal the difficulties, surprises and opportunities that we found in the course of the implemented actions. Finally, we will present a set of primary conclusions, explain what will be our main contribution to the research area and propose an overview on future developments.
