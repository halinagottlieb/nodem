Touch the visitor? Experiences from developing a mobileguide system in Norway
Date added:  2013-10-15 20:52:55
Modified:        2013-10-15 20:57:11
Author / Creator Anette Rattfelthttp://www.laternavox.se
Today many people carry advanced media players in their pockets, smartphones! It is possible to communicate with sounds and pictures, on the spot almost everywhere, but there is a challenge in ”reaching” the visitor. What information should we offer? How shall we design it or introduce it? The Norwegian company Laterna Vox AS has developed a own system, hardware and software, a device/a box. It is a Wifi system that broadcasts sound, texts and images to smartphones within a limited area. It is free to use and requires no app. This system is called, LaternaGuide. The company also create multimedia content. It can be accessed via Internet or via this system, LaternaGuide. Their project started three years ago with Lindesnes lighthouse-museum in Norway and Luleå Technical University in Sweden. Today they have produced stationary mobile guides for 20 different sites, in Norway, Sweden and Finland, in cooperation with museums, communities and other clients. We will tell about our experiences and will demonstrate the guide in the exhibition area.

    Anette Rattfelt

    Producer and CEO, Laterna Vox in Norway / I have worked 10 years as a radiojournalist at Swedish Radio and 10 years in museums with documentation, historical films and collaborative projects, mostly at VLM. Laterna Vox has developed LaternaGuide a system to present information via a smartphone. First one at a site 2011.
