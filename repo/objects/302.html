Taranaki Culture - Fresh out of the box
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:56:24
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:56:25
Author / Creator Eva Artinger
Organization/company: The HIT Lab NZ / University of Applied Sciences Hagenberg Submitted by: Eva Artinger Website: http://www.youchangetheexhibition.co.cc/ The project "You change the exhibition" is a Museum 2.0 add-on for the cultural exhibition Taranaki Culture - Fresh out of the box developed by the HIT Lab NZ for the Puke Ariki museum, during an exchange semester, initiated by the University of Applied Sciences Hagenberg, Austria.  "You change the exhibition" visualizes the interest of the museum visitors in each exhibition object. People can show their interest when they visit the exhibition at the museum, but also when they visit the website of the exhibition. The exhibition objects at the museum are placed on semitransparent stands, which contain lights. The light in the stands change intensity depending on the interest of the visitor in the object, glowing brighter or dimmer the more or less the visitor pays attention to the object. The project helps a new visitor to get an impression about what other people find interesting. Depending on this information the visitor can choose what objects he will look at first. The visitor can then decide if he agrees with what other people think about the objects.
