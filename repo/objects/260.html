Promoottori
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:41:07
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:41:21
Museum/site: Helsinki University Museum Country: Finland Developers: The concept and system: Eero Hyvönen (director), Samppa Saarela, Kim Viljanen Avril Styrman Content provision: Jaana Tegelberg, Kati Heinämies Sponsor partnerships: National Museum, Espoo City Museum, Lahti City Museum , Foundation of Helsinki University Museum, Antikvaria Group consisting of some 20 Finnish museums, Nokia Corp TietoEnator Corp, National Technology Agency Tekes Submitted by: University of Helsinki, Helsinki Institute for Information Technology (HIIT) Project website: http://www.cs.helsinki.fi/group/seco/ Project status: completed Project type: Stationary/Fixed media Start year: 2002 End yearL 2003 Summary: Promoottori is a new kind of photograph retrieval system by which the user can easily find photographs based on what they depict (i.e., content). Searching and browsing the collections is based on concepts and semantics instead of keywords, as usual in current systems. Intentions: Intended for ordinary museum visitors. Useful for professionals, as well. Tech spec: The system is based on the new semantic web technology. A summary of the main ideas is included, as well as a longer paper descibing the technology and usage of the system. Comments: The attached file contains a revised version of a presentation and demonstration at the international SEPIA Conference, Helsinki, Sept. 18-20, 2003. In addition, a 2-page summary of the main ideas is included. The system is in daily use at the Helsinki University Museum, Helsinki.
