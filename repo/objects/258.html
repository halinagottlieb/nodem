Hokusai Manga
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:37:15
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:37:35
Museum/site: Far Eastern Museum Country: Sweden Developers: Touch and Turn AB Inedalsgatan 21 112 33 Stockholm Sponsor partnerships Submitted by: Östasiatiska Museet/The Museum of Far Eastern Antiquities Project website: http://www.touchandturn.com Project status: completed Project type: Stationary/Fixed media Start year: 2003 End year: 2004 Summary: Hokusai Manga is a serie of sketchbooks made by the japanese artist Hokusai, published between 1814 -1878 In connection with the exhibition ”Manga – from Hokusai to Dragonball”, the Museum of Far Eastern Antiquities in Stockholm and the company Touch and Turn has produced a virtual version of Hokusai’s Manga Book. It was Hokusai who first used the word manga which might mean pictorial whims. Today it designaes japanese comics. Intentions: Through the used technology the Manga book, which will be on display in the exhibition, can be accessed in the most realistic way, giving the user a sensation of turning the original book pages, zooming in fine details and reading explicative facts, comments and translations in English and Swedish. The application encourages a dialogue and interactivity, while the original is safely preserved. The whole idea with the exhibition and the digital presentation is to bring together two groups of the museum audience: the young audience, to whom contemporary manga and digital applications are wellknown, and an older audience who admire Hokusai. Hopefully the CD can capture the interest of both of these two groups. The user also has the possibility to buy the "digital facsimile" as CD-ROM Edition or access it on an Internet Virtual Library. (www.touchandturn.com). Tech spec: The T&T technology consists of a software (Publisher) with a user friendly interface (Viewer) for virtual browsing. The Publisher makes it possible to create interactive books for exhibitions, education, research or private use. Stability and performance is ensured by working with standard formats for text and images. The published Manga book is presented on a touch screen as a part of an exhibition. It also comes as a CD ROM Edition. Additionally it will be downloadable from the T&T Virtual Library (www.touchandturn.com). Comments: The production can actually qualify in both the cathegory 3 and 4, however the digital exhibition is its main function. The application follows with a reference demo of the Mangabook in CD Rom.
