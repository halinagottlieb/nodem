Expanding the Museum Experience: The Case of "Þingeyskur Sögugrunnur"
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:44:30
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:44:30
Author / Creator Sigurjón Baldur Hafsteinsson
Author / Creator Daníel Borgþórsson
Organization/company: District Cultural Center, Húsavík Presenter: Sigurjón Baldur Hafsteinsson and Daníel Borgþórsson Type of project: Exhibition In recent years museums around the world have introduced new kind of experience to their target audiences, one based on GIS (Geographic Information System). GIS enables these institutions to enhance the experience within its walls in several different ways; it also enables its audiences to enjoy the museum from without. In this paper, we will talk about a GIS project undertaken in 2004 by The District Cultural Center (DCC) in Húsavík. The DCC incorporates several diverse museums such as a natural history museum, a folk museum, an art museum, a maritime museum, a photographic collection and an archive. The project is called "Þingeyskur sögugrunnur" (Regional Historical Database), and has as its aim not only the mapping of local historical documents and items already preserved in its collections, but also the discovery of additional information (oral stories, documents, artifacts) in diverse communities of fishermen, farmers, and other sectors it has serviced over time. In this presentation, we will talk about our plans to implement the project within DCC´s museum walls, as well as how we plan to make it available to the local eco-tourism industry.
