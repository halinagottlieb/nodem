Exploring CARTA MARINA
Date added:  2013-12-31 10:57:00
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:37:51
Museum/site: Harkko Museum Country: Finland Developers: Systems of Representation research group Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: University of Art and Design, Media Lab Project website: http://cipher.uiah.fi Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2002 End year: 2004 Summary: Exploring CARTA MARINA is a cultural heritage forum in which visitors can learn about Nordic Heritage through storytelling and historical artefacts. A cultural heritage forum is a website (in some cases strongly linked to an actual place) concerned with aspects of cultural heritage and which allows all those interested in its contents to explore, learn, interact with and add information to the site. The Forum - which has its physical location at the Harkko Museo in Raisio, Finland - is being built around the narratives of the Carta Marina of 1539 by Olaus Magnus. At the moment the Forum contains digital cultural heritage in the form of primary sources and interpretive materials as well as toolkit with resource organization, navigation and visualization tools for the WWW. Intentions It is the goal of the Forum to promote community building through education and museum activities. In these activities, the contents of the forum are used to build narratives that educate and make citizens aware of continued relevance of cultural heritage. As an online cultural heritage application, the Forum is anchored to its institution, the Harkko museum. As such it is meant to support, integrate and promote activities for the different communities that interact with the museum. These communities include the schools in the Raisio district that benefit from the services of the museum. During the years 2003 and 2004, the Forum is supporting educational activities in two primary schools in Raisio. In the Ihala and Friisila schools in Raisio, two educators have been utilizing and testing the contents of the Forum as part of the grades 5 and 6 curriculum in Finnish language and art education. The teachers have been using the tools created for the Forum and which include Digital Carta Marina. This is the first interactive version of the Carta Marina online. The tool has enabled the teachers and the students to explore the history and geography of the Nordic region. Drawing inspiration from this map, the students have also been the Soft Ontology Layer (SOL) tool to create an ontology of contemporary monsters. Scholars are also using the tools in the forum to create soft ontologies from their own data. These soft ontologies, which are low-level descriptions of the features of an artifact entered using natural language, are subsequently visualized through similarity cluster maps. The technique allows for categorization to emerge from the data entered. In this manner the Forum hopes to contribute to a multiple perspective approach to cultural heritage. As a web application, the Forum offers high quality content about cultural heritage that can be of interest to communities of experts and the general public. Tech spec: JAVA plugin needed for visualizations Comments: Exploring CARTA MARINA is part of CIPHER, a multidisciplinary, multinational research and development project.
