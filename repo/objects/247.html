Vitlycke Digital guide
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:08:15
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:13:58
Museum/site: Vitlycke Museum Country: Sweden Developers: INSITE INCENTIVE AB Sponsor partnerships: Västra Götalands Regionen Submitted by: Vitlycke Museum Anita Larsson Modin (anita.larsson.modin@bohusmus.se) Project website: http://vitlycke.bohusmus.se/bohusmus/www-vitlycke/ http://www.insiteint.com Project status: Project type: Portable/Wireless Application Start year: 2003 End year: 2004 Summary: The World heritage area at Vitlycke in Tanum plays an important role on the international arena. Due to the Vitlycke Digital Guide it is now possible to attract more international visitors and present the historical information in languages such as Swedish, German and English even though real personal guides are not available. Intentions: The main purpose is to utilize existing as well as future technologies to enhance knowledge transfer and increase the overall attraction value to Vitlycke Museum in regards to Rock Carvings as well as the Bronze Age. The goal is to give the users an emotional experience by introducing a new type of interactive media where the users can choose what information they want at any given time. Tech spec 50 Chapters of content 3 Languages 14 Music pieces 120 min of speaker audio Re-usable content Can be altered for usage in other types of media distribution: CD, DVD, Internet and 3G Development of further functions, GPS navigation, GPRS data transfer and positioning. Comments; This version is the first step of three planned steps. The second step will include the Bronze-age village and the third step will include the whole World heritage area in Tanum (50 square miles) with added functionality as GPS positioning. The continued development is decided and funded after step one is evaluated properly and considered a success.
