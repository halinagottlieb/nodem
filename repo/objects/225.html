The King's Kunstkammer
Date added:  2013-12-31 11:08:07
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:37:16
Museum/site: The National Museum of Denmark / www.natmus.dk Country: Denmark Developers: Bente Gundestrup, Patricia Leeson, Tine Wanning Sponsor partnerships Submitted by: The National Museum of Denmark Project website: http://www.kunstkammer.dk Project status: completed Project type Start year: 2000 End year: 2003 Summary: Intentions: The target group is the generally interested lay museum visitor of all ages. The interactive part "Your own Kunstkammer" is expected to appeal mostly to the younger generation and to be used in educational environments. Tech spec The essence of the project is a database where information concerning the objects, i.e. name, description, provenance, date, material, measure, artist, and literature, is available. Comments: This site was published first in Danish March 2002 and then in August 2003 in English
