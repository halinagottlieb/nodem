Det digitala Bomarsund / Digital Bomarsund
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:33:07
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:33:08
Museum/site: Ålands Museibyrån Country: Finland Developers: Ålands Museibyrån Åland Teknologi Centrum Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Ålands Museibyrån Project website: -- Project status: completed Project type: Stationary/Fixed media Start year: 2001 End year 2003 Summary: The Russian Empire during the early 1800s planned and began to build a huge fortification complex on the Åland Islands at Bomarsund. The scale of the complex was matched by the architectural grandeur of the individual buildings. When war came to Ålands shores in 1854 the entire complex was destroyed. Today the ruins are an important tourist destination but the sense of monumentality is lost. In order to understand the history of Bomarsund, its place in the development of the Russian Empire and the eventual failure of this scheme, it is important that visitors have the opportunity to see what Bomarsund once looked like. The problem: the ruins of Bomarsund are an essential part of the modern Åland identity. Following the war of 1854-56 Åland was demilitarised and is still demilitarised today. Any attempt to rebuild or reconstruct in Bomarsund could compromise this symbolism. The answer: reconstruct the original buildings in a computer environment. The project goal was to create digital models of the four completed defensive buildings at Bomarsund. These would be used in a wide variety of ways to stimulate interest in Bomarsund, both the place and its history. The picture material created by this project is easily accessible, it does not need reference to complex architectural terminology to be able to understand it; the pictures do not need to be translated and they can be interpreted by visitors of all ages. "A picture speaks a thousand words!" Intentions: Internet: the models have been used to create a large number of still pictures and a number of short animations. These will be used on the new homepage to capture the imagination of the "virtual" visitor. The models have been placed into todays landscape. These pictures will be used at the respective locations to help visitors to Bomarsund visualise the original structures. The ruins today are difficult to interpret and provide very little food for the imagination. A film sequence is currently being developed using the computer models. This film will be used within the museum exhibition at Bomarsund and within the main exhibition of Ålands Museum. Postcards and posters will be printed using the new material. The pictures will also be used in brochures and other publications. It is also intended to develop this material for use in schools on Åland and even on the mainland of Finland, if there is an interest. The primary goal is to stimulate the visitors and the potential visitors interest in Bomarsund. This in turn can lead to a more thorough appreciation of the place and its history. Tech spec: The following material was used to develop the 3D models: Russian engineers drawings painting and sketches 19th century photographs information from archaeological excavations The architectural drawings were scanned in to AutoCad where precise measurements were taken and used to build up the various architectural components of each building. These were then imported into 3D Studio Max where they were combined to form the 3D structure. Surfaces were added to the structures, both internal and external. Short animations were made using the final model. The model was also exported from 3D Studio into Photoshop, where it was cut into digital photos of todays landscape. Comments: This production is currently being implemented: the homepage is under construction and due to be online beginning May 2004. Presentation panels are also being developed and are due to be placed in the Bomarsund area early May 2004. A film sequence is being developed (8-10 minutes) and is to be premiered at the film festival, Vera, here on Åland, 10 March 2004. The animations are large in file size and so cannot be sent with this submission. A number of still pictures representing the Main Fort at Bomarsund have been included.
