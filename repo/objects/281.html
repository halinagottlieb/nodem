Hidden Treasure Trove
Date added:  2014-02-02 11:48:20
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:12:00
Author / Creator Mariana Salgado
Author / Creator Juhani Tenhunen
Organization/company: Media Lab - University of Art and Design Helsinki (TAIK), Finland Presenters: Mariana Salgado and Juhani Tenhunen Type of project: On-line and on-site This project proposal describes a concept for open-air museums called Hidden Treasure Trove. The hypothesis is that organizing role-playing sessions with museums visitors and perhaps with especially invited role-players could activate visitors' engagement with the collection both onsite and online. We have observed that visitors to cultural heritage sites can generate interesting material related to the exhibition during the time that the exhibition is on the gallery. The aim of this endeavor is to explore how visitors' generated content, in such forms as audio, videos, texts and images, can be shared and distributed by social media tools, and possibly broadcasted. The project aims to implement co-designing practices by a collaborative process within museum staff, visitors, role-players, and external researchers. Because this is a preliminary description of a future venture, the conclusions are predictions based on our analysis of the potential of the project. We propose new practices that could bring visibility and popularity to the museum and the virtual communities related to it. As a consequence, new learning, inclusive and business opportunities could emerge.

    Mariana Salgado

    Mariana Salgado is an interaction design-researcher. She has a doctoral degree from the Media Lab, University of Art and Design Helsinki. She has worked eight years at the Media Lab Helsinki, during which time she has been collaborating with museums and other cultural organizations, and has actively published in her field of research. Mariana Salgado is also involved in Open Glam initiative.
