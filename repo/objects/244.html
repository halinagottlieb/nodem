IDmessage - mobile Stories
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:02:34
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:02:49
Museum/site: Svenska kyrkan i Malmö/Lund Country: Sweden Developers: IDmessage AB Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: IDmessage AB Project website: http://www.IDmessage.se Project status: completed Project type: Portable/Wireless Application Start year: 2002 End year: 2003 Summary: All objects, people and places have a Story - IDmessage makes it mobile. Based on several granted patents the IDmessaging service can, in s any recipient any time. In one application the mobile phone is used as a tool to establish a connection between the -object- and an interest form for the mobile Story. It can be read in a 3G phone or for free at a broadband connection at home or at the office. Visitors to the old cemeta Lund, can use the IDmesage service to request mobile Stories about some 20 differerent persons. A unique sign is placed at each grave. See sculptures in public places in central Malmö, where a visitor with the aid of IDmessage can request more information. Intentions In a couple of years people will expect to be able to gain access to rich media information and, where relevant, establish communication based of interest. The user shall be presented a choice between various modes of interaction. Variables will be presentation quality, time shifting, tec others. Tech spec: The present application asks the interested person to send an SMS with a Story -ID-. Instructions are on each sign. Our customers are the info people can also - for free - send mobile Stories via our PC application - The StorySender or one of our PDA or smart phone StorySender appli continously developing. Future use of RFID-technology and other developments will further increase ease of use. Comments: IDmessage started in 1997. The IDmessage concept is a general application for requesting and sending mobile Stories. It is a commercial serv varirty of fields. The IDmessage technology and service is today used in advertisments, on business cards, on packages...
