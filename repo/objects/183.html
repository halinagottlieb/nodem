The Wall
Date added:  2013-10-18 13:17:35
Modified:        2013-10-18 13:24:02
Author / Creator Sarah Giersing
Author / Creator Sofie Linde
THE WALL is a multitouch multimedia installation, that brings the museum to where new users are: In the streets. Everybody passing by can explore and interact with the digitized collections, make comments and upload their own images – sharing their views and shaping the interpretation of the city’s past and present.
