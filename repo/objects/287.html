Communication for all
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:16:24
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:23:33
Author / Creator Thomas Ulrich
Author / Creator Torhild Skåtun
Organization/company: Norwegian Telecom Museum, Norway Presenters: Thomas Ulrich and Torhild Skåtun Type of project: Exhibition Website: www.telemuseum.no - click on kommende utstillinger In our new temporary exhibit "Communication for all" (Kommunikasjon for alle), The Norwegian Telecom Museum aims to present new technology providing access to communication technology for groups of people to whom the regular devices have an unfortunate design for handling. Facing reduced functional capacity occurs in the lifespan of most people, so encountering obstacles due to this permanent or temporary condition will be an experience for most people. Historically the problem has been applied to the individual, but recently the trend is to look for the problem in the ways environment and equipment is designed. With our new exhibit we are taking this latter point of view.

    Torhild Skåtun
