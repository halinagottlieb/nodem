Meaning Making Experience by partnership and creative collaboration
Date added:  2013-10-14 23:08:35
Modified:        2013-10-14 23:13:23
Author / Creator Mie Marie Vinther Ellekildemmex.dk
Meaning Making Experience (MMEx) is a pioneering and independent organization with a mission to support the digital communication, creation and innovation in museums - both on a practical and an experimental level. The organization itself is created on the basis of a collaboration between the Development-Council of the Museums in Central Denmark (Midtjyske Museers Udviklingsråd), the Central Denmark Region and interdisciplinary stakeholders, among there audiovisual companies, the University of Aarhus and theaters. What MMEx does: By focusing on the project process and strengthen the museums during idea development, contract negotiations, project managing and the subsequent assessment, MMEx works to render more museums capable of creating engaging, entertaining and educational digital experiences. A fundamental part of MMEx’s objective is that all museums must have free access to all knowledge created within the boundaries of the organization. The knowledge MMEx participate in generating happens through projects and different creative processes, with the common nominator that there always is a partnership or a collaboration of different academic and professional competencies involved. An example on such a partnership, facilitated by MMEx, is Mapping the Archive. Here many different disciplines are launched and challenged across proficiencies (digital, museum, dramaturgical, educational and more). In this experiment the participants are from a museum of cultural history, of natural history, an art museum, an audiovisual company, a theatre and a Ph.D. candidate. The partnership’s goal is to develop a visual user-interface that allows the museums’ knowledge and extensive collections to merge in an intuitive and associative way that can accommodate the different kind of users (the browser, the searcher, the researcher). For much the same reasons MMEx collaborate with the annual “Hack4Dk”, where software developers are invited to explore and work with open cultural data such as images, maps, aerial photos, listed buildings, films and artworks.

    Mie Marie Vinther Ellekilde

    Mie Marie Vinther Ellekilde has a MA in History from the University of Aalborg. Furthermore Mie Ellekilde holds a diploma in Museum and Leisure Management. Past employments include the Historical Museum of Northern Jutland, where she worked as a project assistant on several projects, both analogue and digital, and the Museum of Southern Jutland as curator at the agricultural department. Mie has been a Project Manager at Meaning Making Experience since the beginning of 2013 where she among other things is responsible for all web communication (website and social media) and various digital projects.
