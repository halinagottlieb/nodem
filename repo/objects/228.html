Museum/site: Tekniska Museet/The National Museum of Science and Technology Country: Sweden Developers: Tekniska Museet, Valtech Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Tekniska Museet/The National Museum of Science and Technology Project website: http://www.tekniskamuseet.se Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2002 End year: 2003 Summary: A 3-month pre study was followed by 6 months of intensive web production. Project management, web design and content production was carried out internally. Adaptation of the Content Management System and optimised ranking at search engines was hired from external consultants. The web site was launched the 22nd August 2003. The daily work with publishing information at the web site has been delegated to 12 editors, supported by an editor in chief. Each editor is responsible for his/her own field, adapting the contents of text and image to the end users needs. The web site is designed for end users with low end technical equipment, that is, a regular household pc and internet connection. To support e.g. temporary exhibitions, more technically – and graphically -advanced web sites can be designed. It will then be published on a server of its own. Intentions Our goal is that the user may carry out a number of tasks at the web site: Prepare for an upcoming visit at the physical museum. Search facts about technical and industrial history. Search in our extensive databases of the museum’s objects. Find more and less advanced experiments to carry out at school or at home. Find information for teachers and students alike before, during and after their visit at the physical museum. Find press information. Subscribe to news letters. Shop on line. Find information about the museum’s archive, objects and library. Tech spec: The web is published on its own server. The CMS (Content Management System) used is EpiServer 4. One SQL Server database for collections. Comments: The web site is designed to be an extension of the operations of The National Museum of Science and Technology. The web site contains information about current exhibitions, as well as in depth information which for various reasons cannot be available
