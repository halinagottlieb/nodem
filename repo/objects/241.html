Museum/site: (Rogaland Kunstmuseum, Norge) Country: Denmark Developers: Artist Lisa Rosenmeier, Musiscian Lennart Ginman, Technic Rune Baess Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Postal address: Grøndalsvej 24 2000F Denmark Project website: http://www.houseoflove.dk Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 1999 End year: 2001 Summary: Intro to www.houseoflove.dk House of Love or plotting the field for the unfolding of a possible love around the year 2000... www.houseoflove.dk is a project focusing on the concept of love. This multi-faceted work explores the ways in which the projection of this concept is manifested in contemporary culture. House of Love takes its point of departure in one of the key narratives of our time - the myth of the great passion, exemplified by the fatal love story of Elvira Madigan and Sixteen Sparre. House of Love is a visual, auditory and textual "map" of the concepts associated with the great passion - Longing, Meeting, The Other, Death, The Unknowable. This project is in many ways a cultural-historical study of the phenomenon of love - a kind of status report on love in the year 2002. Moving through a series of translocations the artistic project House of Love consists of three phases, each in a different kind of public space. In the first phase the myth of love was presented on advertisements on buses and billboards and at a café in Copenhagen. In the second phase it was exhibited at an art museum in Norway, while the third phase, the website www.houseoflove.dk, not only documents the two preceding phases but also functions as an artistic project in itself. www.houseoflove.dk maps all of these different aspects through multiple visual and auditory segments. They branch out in many different directions but are nevertheless visually linked in a network in which each node constitutes a new centre, giving the viewer the autonomy of choosing his or her own "line" through the project.
