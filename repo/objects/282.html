The Town Chronicler
Date added:  2014-02-02 11:50:48
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:11:56
Author / Creator Torben Holm
Organization/company: Struer Museum, Sweden Presenter: Torben Holm Type of project: Interactive exhibition installation The Town Chronicler was a phenomenon that began in the Middle Ages. Here the Royal Appointee would handle and record the legal and financial documentation of the local community. At the Museum of Struer, The Town Chronicler is still collecting and documenting. Replacing books and quills with an interactive touchscreen, a multimedieal supporting database and online access, the Town Chronicler at the Museum af Struer both presents and collects the history of the area. Visitors are able to browse the database simply by dragging the timeline presented on the screen. Information is placed in chronological order in the form of articles or pictures, and touching presents the relevant information. As part of an exhibition on local history, The Town Chronicler offers a high degree of interactivity. Supporting audio as well as video, The Town Chronicler delivers a wide ragne of information in several medias. The system enables not only the choice of information that the exhibition space can contain, but also the subject and period. Ideally, a user-generated database, based to a large extent on information provided by visitors, creating a two-way communication system thus enabling our guests to actively contribute both to the exhibition and their own local history.
