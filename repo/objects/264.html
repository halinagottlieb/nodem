Ølstykke gennem tiderne - en 3d visualisering
Date added:  2013-12-31 13:48:44
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:48:51
Museum/site: Skenkelsø Mølle Museum Country: Denmark Developers: idea & production: Rolf Kjær-Hansen; Models, textures, renders: Jonas Højgaard; Landscape digitizing: Johnny Ahlgren; Technical support: 3d Connection Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Skenkelsø Mølle Museum/Ølstykke Kommune Project website: -- Project status: completed Project type: Stationary/Fixed media Start year: 2002 End year: 2004 Summary: 3d computer aided visualization of the development of landscape and habitation and the interaction as seen over time - from the stone age -4.000 bc, to agrarian society ad 1850, post enclosure movement. The depicted area approx. 3.000 hectars covers the present day area of Ølstykke county counsil Intentions: Stand alone interactive graphic presentation. Target audience and focus group primarily school children. Exhibit is mobile and can be placed at schools or others venues for interactive and self-explanatory use. Tech spec: Graphic presentation: Modelling: Discreet-3d studio MAX; Autodesk-AutoCAD. Rendering: avi-files employing DivX codec. Presentation shell: html/java/flash Comments:
