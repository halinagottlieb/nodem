Bringing alive early flying with 3D technology
Date added:  2013-10-18 12:07:05
Modified:        2014-01-06 19:47:12
Author / Creator Marie Ørstedholm
Author / Creator Louise Skyggebjerg
In an apparently traditional showcase a 3D story visualizing the history of flying suddenly appears as a dreamlike vision above the objects. Our project presentation focuses on the process of creating the installation from the first ambitious ideas to the result, which reflects technical and economic constraints of the project.
