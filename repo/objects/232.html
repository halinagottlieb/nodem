Separation/Identity/Society
Date added:  2013-12-31 11:45:41
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:36:20
Museum/site Country: Sweden Developers: Iiris Liliendal, Digitalbridge, Creative Producer, Sweden Vincent Eaton, Belgium Virigina Vravaio, Ravenna museums, Italy Joe Lambert, founder of Digital Storytelling Center, USA David Casacuberta, www.transit.es, Spain Erik Tirkkonen, Vapriikki museum Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: Digitalbridge Project website: http:// Project status: idea Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2003 End year: 2006 Summary: The project Separation/Identity/Society makes it possible for ordinary European people to learn to make a short film about their memories of separation by computer. They can use photos, drawings, letters, everything we can scan and the voice of their own to tell their story. The films are made in 4-5 days workshops in every country in the native languages. There are going to be about 140 digital stories on the website/CD-ROM/DV/DVD. The aim of the project is to show that we and our memories are one part of the Modern European History. We all are influenced by the history. The European organisations participating are for instance Vapriikki (Finland), Ravenna museums (Italy), War Child Archive (England) Intentions: To raise awareness of European people that they and their individual memories are a part of the common European history. To raise awareness how historical occurences as a war have an effect many generations. ...to thrill those who look and listen with the notion that they, too, might have a voice. Tech spec: The digital films are made at a workshops by computers. We are going to use program as Adobe Photosho, Adobe Premiere or iMovie by Macintosh. The films can be seen on Internet/CD-ROM/DV/DVD. Comments: The project is also nr. 3 Stationary/Fixed Media
