Project Access: Digitizing a Museum Collection
Date added:  2014-02-02 11:31:23
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:12:10
Author / Creator Johanna Forsberg
For a few years the Army Museum has worked with publishing our collections on the Internet. The work is a multi layer process and is the result of a systematic inventorying project that began in 2006. The regular museum staff has worked alongside temporarily hired staff to bring the project to an end. Our goal is to have a complete inventory of the museum's collection by the end of 2008 and to be the first museum to publish an entire museum collection online. To reach this goal we had to look at the big picture. We couldn't allow ourselves to get bogged down in the details. We have chosen a few fields from the database that we believe is of general interest to publish on the Internet. More effort has been put on making those fields public than others. Therefore our presentation online is meant more as an overview than a detailed academic record. This method will allow us to reach a wider audience since we believe many online viewers will find our level of knowledge to be of a satisfying level. It is our intent to spur interest in the novice and further inquiry from the expert. We also believe that by using this technique we will reach our goal of a complete digital record of our museums entire inventory by the end of 2009
