Trial of “MoSaIC”: Museum of Shared and Interactive Cataloguing
Date added:  2014-01-31 09:35:44
Modified:        2014-01-31 09:41:20
Author / Creator Hiroyo Ishikawa
Author / Creator Kunitake Kaneko
Our vision of digital museum is to have many museums connected over network and share their digitized heritage among them. In the vision, the digital museum allows users to access to the digital contents on demand and helps users to understand them deeply by using polymorphic contexts, and encourages users to discover unseen digital contents. We have tried to build such a digital museum by modeling and cataloguing relations of digital contents, by sharing them as an autonomous distributed database, and by communication network technologies. To build the digital museum, the following techniques are required; * a technique to model and catalogue digital contents relationships * a technique to store a large number of digital data and access to shared digital contents smoothly * a technique to find unnoticed relationships among contents by using polymorphic visualization providing an interactive interface. In NODEM2013, we showed a prototype of the digital museum named “MoSaIC II” which is an interactive polymorphic visualizing system using 4K touch displays. This system visualizes Catalogues representing the relationships among digital contents. Assuming somebody has created Catalogues by linking digital contents with mutual relationships, we visualize the Catalogues in a window using contents themselves and arrows representing relations in virtual 3D world interactively and realize the polymorphic visualization from multi directions. When a user chooses a content node, the content appears in another window with detail and unseen arrows and contents pointing to or from the content come up around the selected content node. The number of visualized contents and Catalogues increase according to the user’s content choices, and the appearance also changes gradually.
