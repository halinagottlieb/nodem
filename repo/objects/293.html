Brede Works - Museum of industrial culture
Date added:  2014-02-02 12:31:35
Modified:        2014-02-02 12:31:35
Author / Creator Mikkel Thelle
Organization/company: National Museum of Denmark Presenter: Mikkel Thelle Type of project: Exhibition Following a vision from the 1950's, the National Museum is developing a new museum for industrial culture, Brede Works, north of Copenhagen. One of the intentions is to let the users choose, discuss and explore the information and material put forward, and this will partly be achieved through digital and interactive media. Following the complexity of the theme, industrial culture of Denmark, the museum concept comprises very different elements. Some of the digital elements are: interactive films, hands-on factory activities, blog-on-the-wall, film wallpapers, soundscapes and others. To keep a digital spine throughout the entire complex, an Active Ticket is developed to let the visitor collect, explore and share the exhibition content. The project has been going on for years, and the last part of it has been including a number of external partners, while many processes has been in play simultaneously. With external help, the core group of the project has been guided through a development process in different stages.
