AudioFaktor
Date added:  2013-10-18 12:32:45
Modified:        2013-10-18 12:37:41
Author / Creator Jacob Westergaard Madsen
Author / Creator Henrik Lynbech
Together with Redia, Danmarks Rockmuseum is developing a new interactive product called AudioFaktor. At its present stage AudioFaktor enables users to remix music on a touch screen by using the screen as a large iPhone – each instrument in a song is represented by a circle which can be manipulated in various ways to alter the music (see video link below). AudioFaktor has great potential, in that it can be used to play with and remix existing songs from the Danish popular music history, it can be used for creating new music, making beats etc. – and it requires no musical skills other than curiosity. Danmarks Rockmuseum aims at becoming a place where historical documentation and learning is combined with playing and a physical sense of the music. AudioFaktor fits perfectly with the visions of the museum. The further development of the activity can aim at a total integration with the historical content in the exhibition area – the music you are remixing will be communicated with references to the artefacts etc on the artist in question. Or the user can be brought straight to the digitalised collection where information in the artist can be found. Or other uses can be thought of – the next years will show where it goes. User inputs will be of great importance. We propose a) a project presentation on AudioFaktor (15-20 min) to demonstrate the technical background, present the cooperation between Danmarks Rockmuseum and Redia, discuss its possible future development and b) a presentation in the exhibition area to let conference participants have ‘hands on’ and try the activity. We consider both presentations to be part of the further development of the project, in that we gather valuable user inputs. Also, the exhibition presentation will bring a playful element to the conference.

    Jacob Westergaard Madsen

    Curator at Danmarks Rockmuseum

    Henrik Lynbech

    Interactive designer at Redia (Alexandra Inst.)
