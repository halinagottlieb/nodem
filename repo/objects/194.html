Sounds of Bornholm – a prototype of a digital museum for Danish audio culture
Date added:  2013-10-18 15:22:51
Modified:        2013-10-23 00:05:22
Author / Creator Stephen Schwartzhttp://soundsfromdenmark.org
Underway: Scandinavia´s first virtual Museum for Audio Culture, with on-line workshop for user generated audio content, an archive of sound pictures, captured sonic moments, recordings of sounds – and an audio community. This Museum will be a social experiment, involving local communities in focusing on audio that defines their cultural identities.

    Stephen Schwartz

    Sound artist and innovator, journalist, lecturer, SoundTales, Denmark. Sound artist, radio feature journalist, born: 1940, in Detroit, Michigan and immigrated to Denmark, 1961. Production experience: Internship at Danmarks Radio (1963 -66) under the Master of Danish Sound Pictures, Willy Reunert. Permanent staff member in Cultural and Current Affairs Department, DR in 1967 as a radio feature producer and developer of “radiophonic expression”. Producer of the series: ”The Family of Man” – portraits drawn with the microphone. Initiator of DRs Tape Workshop, 1969. Producer of radio montages (Prix Italia winner in radio documentaries, 1982 and 1992). Internationally known for form innovation. Pensioned from DR 2002. Teaching experience: 40 years teaching of audio expression and Danish audio. Culture, feature production methods, storytelling techniques, dramaturgi in DR and NRK. Internationally known lecturer and teacher of audio expression and didactics. Co-author of book: ”*Radiomontager – lyden der skaber billeder*” (1998, Systime & DRs forlag). Course development in feature production (The Scenario Approach) for UNESCO, EBU and CBC. Audio storytelling course(2006) at Institut for Kunst og Kulturvidenskab, KUA. Development experience: Leader of DRs Feature department (P1) 1969 – 1973, together wirth Erling Kristensen and Mads Bostrup Many experiments with new audio recording techniques (analog og digital). Leader of PUK (DR’s Product Development Department) 1969-1970. DANIDA (Danish Development Aid)– short term consultatnt, 1983, Noakali, Bangladesh. Leader of Podcasting Innovations Laboratorium (PodLab), under ITMedie, at KUA, 2006. Developer of audio games and SoundQuiz (2006). Audio Installations: ”Eccos from the past” – sound and audio stories at Kronborg Castle (2009). Links: Examples of Danish audio culture: “Nå” http://snd.sc/MVQwBs; ”Landgang” http://snd.sc/MVPYvq. Work in progress: *Øresundskoncert* project in the Town of Elsinore; *SoundQuiz *audio game development, with AAU – Communications.
