Tapiola 50 -Architecture, Art and Stories
Date added:  2013-12-31 12:16:04
Modified:        2013-12-31 12:17:50
Museum/site: WeeGee Cultural Centre Country: Finland Developers: The City of Espoo, Arkkitehtitoimisto Livady Valve Group Sponsor partnerships: Submitted by: The City of Espoo Project website: http://www.weegee.fi/tapiola50 Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application: Start year: 2002 End year: 2003 Summary: The foundation stone of Tapiola garden city was laid in 5 September 1953. To celebrate Tapiola's 50th anniversary, a multimedia application Tapiola 50 has been produced to present the architectural points of interest and public artworks in Tapiola, as well as historical maps of Tapiola and stories about it. The multimedia application is a unique experience, providing a completely new angle on Tapiola. During the jubilee year, the application can be viewed as part of Tapiola - in search of a better life exhibition at Espoo City Museum. Intentions: Tech spec: You need a Flash player plug-in to your browser to view the Tapiola 50 -application. Comments: The project has been realised as part of the TaiKu project of the urban programme for the Helsinki metropolitan area. The programme is jointly funded by the Cities of Espoo, Kauniainen, Helsinki and Vantaa as well as the Uusimaa Regional Council.
