"shaping Urban Art pathways (sUAp)" – Almada: a Portuguese Case Study
Date added:  2013-10-16 08:52:35
Modified:        2013-10-16 08:52:35
Author / Creator Helena Ruahttp://www.ist.utl.pt/en/
Author / Creator Ana Toméhttp://www.ist.utl.pt/en/
Author / Creator Ana Isabel Ribeirowww.m-almada.pt/casadacerca
Author / Creator João Madeiras Pereirahttp://www.ist.utl.pt/en/
Author / Creator Ana Paula Falcão Florhttp://www.ist.utl.pt/en/
Almada’s municipality (Portugal), and its demographic characteristics, has played a pioneering role in the identification, characterization, preservation and dissemination of urban art. Given its size, it constitutes an appropriate geographic area for the assessment of how technology can induce and foster the discovery of the urban space under the pretext of the narrative of its identity values. The construction of urban art routes in a virtual environment is significantly more complex than the traditional modelling of museum spaces, for reasons associated with the definition of the paths characteristics and the delivery mode of informative contents. Thus, the route definition will have to consider several factors, such as the terrain orographic specifications and accessibility conditions, instead of the common two-dimensional modelling of least cost paths. The provision of socio-cultural contents (narrative, graphic or pictorial) pertaining to the artistic elements integrated into the urban space (e.g. panels, wall coverings, sculptures, statues, buildings, integrated projects for collective use), through the use of information technologies and communication technologies (ICT), can induces a fuller appreciation of places. In a municipality/university framework, the sUAp project intends to make a contribution to how the urban space can be experienced, by exploring its applicability in three key ways: - disseminating socio-cultural contents, so they can be accessed, consulted and analyzed remotely, based on the virtual model of the urban space; - using tools based on Geo-BIM to promote content editing and customization, real-time viewing both by the users of the model and by the users of the urban space; - implementing actual user experience of the prototype developed in the context of the case study, via the animation of the real space under analysis, in order to promote a more active use of the urban environment by its citizens.

    Helena Rua

    Helena Rua: 1987 – Graduation in Architecture (Renovation and Preservation of Architecture and Urban Canters), from Faculty of Architecture (FA), Technical University of Lisbon, Portugal. 2004 - PhD in Territorial Engineering, (Thesis: Geographic Information Systems in Detection of Uillæ in Rural Areas in Roman Portugal: a Predictive Model), Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), Technical University of Lisbon, Portugal. 2004 - Assistant Professor of Architecture at IST, Civil Engineering and Architecture Department. Participates in archaeological campaigns since 1986; in collaboration with the German Archaeological Institute, under the guidance of Prof. Theodor Hauschild; with the Cascais Culture Association, under the guidance of Prof. José d’Encarnação; and with the Lisbon County Council, under the guidance of Dr. Guilherme Cardoso. Co-responsible for training workshops of ISTAR – IST Architectural Research Laboratories, since 2010; where she teaches courses in geometry and computer graphics, mainly on the research field of Digital Project Applied to Heritage. Author and co-author of several publications, among which are the following which match the combination of two main research areas - archaeology and computer graphics: • “Geographic Information Systems in Archaeological Analysis: a Predictive Model in the Detection of Rural Roman Villæ“, Journal of Archaeological Science 36 (2009), pp. 224-235. • “Living the past: 3D models, virtual reality and game engines as tools for supporting archaeology and the reconstruction of cultural heritage and the case-study of the Roman villa of Casal de Freiria“, Journal of Archaeological Science 38 (2011), pp. 3296-3308. Her research focuses on virtual reconstruction applied to past scenarios, based on settlement patterns obtained by the surveying of geographic locations.

    Ana Tomé

    Ana Tomé: Assistant Professor at the Department of Civil Engineering, Architecture and Georesources the Technical University of Lisbon (Instituto Superior Técnico – IST/UTL) where she teaches Architectural Design Studio I, Geometric Modeling and Visualization of Buildings and Environmental Design. She holds a PhD in Architecture from IST/UTL, Technical University, December 2010. She is a research member of ICIST (group 6) at IST. She is responsible for the ISTAR laboratories (laboratory of computational architecture and bioclimatic architecture lab). Her research interests are focused in the sustainability area of the built environment oriented to the environment modelling, and space-use modelling, and to the performance simulation techniques (design technologies) within the scope of the life cycle prolongation and the liveability of the built environment.

    Ana Isabel Ribeiro

    Director of the ‘Casa da Cerca’ – Center of Comntemporary Art (Municipality of Almada) since 2002. She holds a BA in History from University of Lisbon – School of Literature (1982). She holds a Master in Contemporary Art History from Social Sciences and Humanities University (1994). She develops work in Arts, Public Art and Architecture research field. She is the author and co-author of more than a hundred texts and papers, published in specialized editions of public art and artistic domain. She regularly participates in international conferences and seminars disclosing the production, management and dissemination activities held by the Municipality of Almada in its public art heritage, which she is the responsible coordinator.

    João Madeiras Pereira

    Associate Professor at the Computer Science Department of the Technical University of Lisbon (Instituto Superior Técnico - IST/UTL) where he teaches Computer Graphics. João Pereira holds a PhD in Electrical and Computers Engineering (Computer Graphics) from IST/UTL, Technical University, December 1996. He received also a MSc and a BsEE degrees in Electrical and Computers Engineering from IST/UTL, respectively in 1989 and 1984. He coordinates the Visualization and Simulation action line of the VIMMI group at INESC-ID (Computer Systems Engineering Institute). His main research fields are Real-Time Rendering, 3D Game Programming, Serious Games, Networked Virtual Environments, Augmented Reality and Parallel Computer Graphics. He won several prizes like the international 2nd Prize at "1994 MasParChallenge Contest" and the national 1st prizes "Best Graphic Interactive Demonstrator" in 2004 and 2002, respectively, with the games "Lost Ages – A Massive Role Playing Game" and "Peace and War Games: Large Scale Simulation Over the Internet". He has been involved with several European (RESOLV, IMPROVE, RTP11.13, MAXIMUS, SATIN, INTUTION, TARGET, GALA) and National projects He was also proposal evaluator of the FET during 2009. He is author or co-author of more than 100 peer-reviewed scientific papers presented at national and international events and journals. Professor Pereira is member of the Eurographics Association and EuroVR Association.

    Ana Paula Falcão Flor

    Graduated in Geographical Engineering (Lisbon university) and completed her PhD in Territorial Engineering in 2010. The research activity have been developed in the use and exploitation of remote sensing imagery, in the digital elevation models construction for floodplains mapping purpose and in several GIS applications. First steps are being taken in GeoBIM domain.
