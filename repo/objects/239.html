VIRMA
Date added:  2013-12-31 12:33:53
Modified:        2013-12-31 13:53:56
Museum/site Helsinki CIty Museum Country Finland Developers Helsinki City Museum, Meetfactory Elisa Communications Research Sponsor partnerships Submittor Helsinki City Museum Project website: http://www.virtualhelsinki.net/museum/virma/ Project status: completed Project type: Web based/Virtual Application Start year: 2000 End year: 2003 Summary: Museum's team made manuscript, developed 3d scenes and avatars. Html pages and games where made also by museum. Meetfactory made vrml and avatar programmin. Elisa gave the platform. Intentions: Main target group school children, teachers, students and general public. Websites are realized in finnish. Tech spec Interactive 3d and 2d model application in internet. Text pages and games were made in html. 3d world was made in vrlm. The platform is blaxxun. Comments: With 3d model technology we wanted to visualize an ancient quarter in the heart of the city by using the knowledge from the archeological studies made by museum. This 3d model is the only visual document of this quarter. The texts are based on the original report the fire inspector from 1805 and on the archeological report made in 1993 and 1995-96
