Helena Rua
  Helena Rua http://www.ist.utl.pt/en/
Helena Rua: 1987 – Graduation in Architecture (Renovation and Preservation of Architecture and Urban Canters), from Faculty of Architecture (FA), Technical University of Lisbon, Portugal. 2004 - PhD in Territorial Engineering, (Thesis: Geographic Information Systems in Detection of Uillæ in Rural Areas in Roman Portugal: a Predictive Model), Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), Technical University of Lisbon, Portugal. 2004 - Assistant Professor of Architecture at IST, Civil Engineering and Architecture Department. Participates in archaeological campaigns since 1986; in collaboration with the German Archaeological Institute, under the guidance of Prof. Theodor Hauschild; with the Cascais Culture Association, under the guidance of Prof. José d’Encarnação; and with the Lisbon County Council, under the guidance of Dr. Guilherme Cardoso. Co-responsible for training workshops of ISTAR – IST Architectural Research Laboratories, since 2010; where she teaches courses in geometry and computer graphics, mainly on the research field of Digital Project Applied to Heritage. Author and co-author of several publications, among which are the following which match the combination of two main research areas - archaeology and computer graphics: • “Geographic Information Systems in Archaeological Analysis: a Predictive Model in the Detection of Rural Roman Villæ“, Journal of Archaeological Science 36 (2009), pp. 224-235. • “Living the past: 3D models, virtual reality and game engines as tools for supporting archaeology and the reconstruction of cultural heritage and the case-study of the Roman villa of Casal de Freiria“, Journal of Archaeological Science 38 (2011), pp. 3296-3308. Her research focuses on virtual reconstruction applied to past scenarios, based on settlement patterns obtained by the surveying of geographic locations.
