Koula Charitonos
  Koula Charitonos
Koula Charitonos is a Doctoral Student in the Institute of Educational Technology at the Open University, with research interests in the field of mobile learning across formal and informal settings. Her doctoral research investigates the use of social and mobile technologies in schools trips to museums, with a particular focus on using technologies to engage young people in meaning-making encounters with artefacts. Canan Blake is a Research Fellow and the co-convener of Computers and Learning Research Group (CALRG) in the Institute of Educational Technology at the Open University. Her work centres on researching collaborative learning and interaction in computer-supported learning environments, mobile learning and technology-enhanced learning in science and in work places. Eileen Scanlon is a professor of Educational Technology and Associate Director (Research and Scholarship) in the Institute of Educational Technology at the Open University. Professor Scanlon has research interests in the field of inquiry learning, technology-enhanced learning in science and science communication. Ann Jones is a Reader in Educational Technology in the Institute of Educational Technology at the Open University with research interests in mobile learning, social and affective uses of computers and the evaluation and educational uses of ICT.
