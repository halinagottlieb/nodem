Narayan Sharma
  Narayan Sharma
Phd Candidate, School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS), London, UK. Narayan Sharma is a doctoral student of Linguistics in the School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS) at the University of London. His research interests include argument structure, linguistic typology, and language documentation. Audio-Visual recording, use of Toolbox, and ELAN are among his technology skills. He was awarded an ELDP Individual Graduate Scholarship (2010-2013), a Global Supplementary Grants Program (2012-2013), an Overseas Research Student Award (2009-2012), and an ELAP Grant (2009-2010) to work on the Morpho-syntax of Puma. In 2004, he joined the DOBES funded project ‘The Chintang and Puma Documentation Project (CPDP)’ as a Research Assistant for five years. In addition to publication of a number of articles, he is co-editor of Puma-Nepali-English Dictionary (2009) and Gurung-Nepali-English Dictionary (2004).
