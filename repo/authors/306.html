Sten-Åke Sändh
  Sten-Åke Sändh
Sten-Åke Sändh works as an IT Coordinator at the Stockholm City Museum. He has a background as a certified Aircraft Technician and has worked in the graphic design field with manufacturing of printing presses and graphic design machinery. He is a Lecturer and Educator with a degree from Stockholm University and the Institute of Education. He has been a teacher in Social Sciences and Computer/IT for 9 years at Ungdomsskolan. Between 1989 and 2003 he worked as a Product Manager for software and IT Developer for business applications at Esselte Data Soft (later Scribona) the nordic region's largest IT distributor company. Since 2009 he has worked as Developer and Technical Project Manager on the design and development of the Digital Museum in Stockholm.
