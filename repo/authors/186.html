Sheenagh Pietrobruno
  Sheenagh Pietrobruno http://www.sheenaghpietrobruno...
Sheenagh Pietrobruno is a Research Associate at the Simone de Beauvoir Institute at Concordia University in Montreal, Canada. Her research combines digital heritage, new media and digital culture, intangible heritage, performing arts, gender studies and media history. She has received numerous research awards: a Visiting Scholar position at the McGill Institute for the Study of Canada (MISC); a Scientist-in-Residence position at gendup: Centre for Women and Gender Studies at the University of Salzburg; the Muriel Gold Senior Visiting Scholar position at the Institute for Gender, Sexuality and Feminist Studies (IGSF) at McGill University; a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at the Advanced Cultural Studies Institute of Sweden (ACSIS) at Linköping University; and a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at Goldsmiths’, University of London. Her work has been published in leading international journals including the International Journal of Heritage Studies, New Media and Society, the International Journal of Cultural Studies and Early Popular Visual Culture. She is the author of Salsa and Its Transnational Moves (Rowman and Littlefield, 2006). Her forthcoming book is Digital Legacies: The Global Archiving of Intangible Heritage. Conducting research throughout the world – Canada, England, Sweden, Austria and Turkey - has enriched her work with a global perspective.
