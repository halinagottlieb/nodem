Ebbe Strandell
  Ebbe Strandell
Ebbe Strandell is the Chief Technical Officer of ABBA The Museum. He received his MSc in Media Technologies from Linköping University in 2007 and started his career as a Software and System Developer. He worked in Taiwan as Assistant Researcher developing a system to distribute live image data from coral reefs to marine biologists across the globe. Two years later he returned to Sweden where he worked with flight simulators, specifically with communication exercises between pilots and flight controllers. In 2010, Ebbe joined the touring exhibition called Abba World and worked with software development and integration between software and hardware. When the touring exhibition finally found its home in Stockholm, he had a leading role in planning and building the new museum.
