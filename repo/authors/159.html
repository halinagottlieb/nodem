Sara Chiesa
  Sara Chiesa http://www.dipartimentodesign....
Phd student in Design at Design Department of Politecnico di Milano. Master Degree in Museology at the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore di Milano, she works for the Extra-European Collection of the Castello Sforzesco in Milano. Her research interests are focused on design for Museums of World Cultures, ethnographic patrimony and intangibile heritage.
