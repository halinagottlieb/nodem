Fiona McDermott
  Fiona McDermott
Fiona McDermott is a multidisciplinary designer and researcher who is currently engaged as a research assistant on the project meSch (Material Encounters with digital Cultural Heritage) at the interaction Design Centre, University of Limerick. She is a graduate of the Bartlett School of Architecture, University College London (MArch Urban Design) and Queen’s University Belfast (BSc Technology). She has extensive professional experience in the fields of exhibition design, architecture and spatial planning in the UK, Germany and Ireland and her academic interests lie in the construct of narrative environments, co-design and the design of contemporary public space.
