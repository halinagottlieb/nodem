Yoonji Song
  Yoonji Song http://ct.kaist.ac.kr/main.php...
Yoonji Song a master’s degree candidate in the Graduate School of Culture Technology at Korea Advanced Institution of Science and Technology (KAIST). Her major as undergraduate student at University was Mathematics and Culture theory and criticism. She has a work experience in 2012 as an intern for SUUM Project & Academy, which is an exhibition and art education institute, having its headquarter in London since 2003. Her main job at the SUUM was to design the visual art project and to plan the exhibition contents for the Youth Olympic games (YOG) 2012 in Innsbruck. At present, she is in Communicative Interaction lab (C.I.lab). Her main interests lies in how to integrate Humanities, Arts, and Technology. Particularly, she is interested in the interactive educational methods and the museum display for effective education as her future research subject.
