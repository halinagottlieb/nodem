Jesvin Yeo
  Jesvin Yeo
Designer, Educator, Researcher, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Jesvin Yeo is a designer, educator and researcher based in Singapore, and graduated from Central Saint Martins College of Art and Design in London. Her research interests relate to the historical and contemporary cultural changes in the areas of “Asian Material Culture”, “Singapore Studies” and “Typography”. She investigates the intersection of cultural elements and motivation, as well as experiment with communication through visual and technology. Jesvin is an associate editor of Design Principles and Practices: An International Journal and The International Journal of the Book. She also serves as deputy publications coordinator at Singapore Society of Asian Studies. Jesvin’screative works have been exhibited and published internationally, including 798 Art District in Beijing, Museum of Design in Atlanta and the Museum of Contemporary Art in Washington D.C., Applied Typography 22, Wallpaper*, The Design Journal and How Magazine.
