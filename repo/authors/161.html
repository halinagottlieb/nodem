Theis Vallø Madsen
  Theis Vallø Madsen http://www.mailartarchive.com
Theis Vallø Madsen (b. 1980), MA in Art History and a PhD Candidate at Aarhus University working on a research project about mail art, network and museum archives. The basis of the project is Mogens Otto Nielsen’s mail art archive at KUNSTEN Museum of Modern Art Aalborg. His research is concerned with archive and museum studies, avant-garde art and contemporary art, new media theory, network theory, and digital culture and aesthetics. He initiated “Mapping the Archive” – a corporation between Aarhus University, Meaning Making Experience, three Danish museums, three digital designers, and Danish Broadcasting Corporation set out to develop new ways of organizing and visualizing messy art collections.
