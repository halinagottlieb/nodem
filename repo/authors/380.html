Jørn H. Hurum
  Jørn H. Hurum http://www.nhm.uio.no
Jørn Hurum´s research areas are Mesozoic mammals (Multituberculates), Dinosaurs (Tyrannosaurids) and Dinosaur tracks from Svalbard. He has done fieldworks in a several areas, including Mongolia, Canada, Argentina, China and Svalbard. Hurum is particulary known for being an effective communicator and mediator of popular science within his area of expertice, amongst others through his book "The development of mankind" ("Menneskets utvikling") and as a host in “Jørn´s corner” in the TV-show Newton and the radioshow Hurum & Ødegaard. Together with Hans Arne Nakrem and Geir Sørli, he received a price for best communicator of knowledge and science at the Univeristy of Oslo in 2001.
