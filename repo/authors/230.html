Roberto Frasca
  Roberto Frasca www.ettsolutions.com
Roberto Frasca works in ETT S.p.A. since 2008 as a commercial and project manager in the Multimedia and Museum Area.rnAfter his degree in Education Sciences, specialising as an Expert in training processes, he acquired several years’ experience as a project manager in the museum / cultural sector, managing and coordinating the resources involved in projects; contributing with his knowledge and expertise in multimedia and training.Commercial and project manager of various museum exhibitions developed by ETT, notable in the way that they surmount the traditional barriers between people and technology, and the involvement of the visitor in content which offers an "immersive" experience to visitors of all ages. He coordinated, among other projects, the “Nazario Sauro” Multi touch Pre-show, permanent MEM - Memory and Migration exhibition and “4D Storm Chamber” at Galata Sea and Migration Museum in Genoa, the Museum of Rome - Palazzo Braschi, the Turin Planetarium, Astronomy and Space Museum multimedia layout, and the new permanent exhibition dedicated to space and astronomy at the National Museum of Science and Technology in Milan.
