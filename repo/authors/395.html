Susanne Kindeberg
  Susanne Kindeberg
Susanne Kindeberg (MA in Interaction Design & Master of Arts), has been working in the field of Interaction and User Experience (UX) design for many years. She has an extensive experience from the mobile phone industry in Sweden and Japan, but also from the educational sector, developing user-friendly applications. Currently she runs the project Developer in Residence, a collaboration project between the three large museums in south of Sweden, supported by the Regional Council.
