Päivi Nikkola
  Päivi Nikkola
BBA (Intl. business) Päivi Nikkola has 29 years of experience in a variety of responsibilities in travel sector in Finland and abroad. Mrs. Nikkola has gained a wide expertise in international travelling business in the USA as an entrepreneur (IATA travel agency & tour operator, 8 years as a CEO). In Finland tasks have included regional travel development, travel business consulting, marketing, customer service, travel business management and leadership as well as creating travel agency services both for leisure and business clients.
