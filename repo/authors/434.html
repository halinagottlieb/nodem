Claudio Pedica
  Claudio Pedica http://gagarin.is
Claudio Pedica is an Italian creative technologist and interaction designer based in Iceland. With a background in artificial intelligence, he escapes the limitations of functional optimality to design an experience that connects emotionally to the audience. He practices scientific insight, smart design and aesthetic sensibility in the crafting of technological artifacts with a higher degree of human affinity. Before joining Gagarin, Claudio worked in the field of AI research for six years. He introduced the concept of territoriality for interactive characters, an innovative approach grounded on the sociological theories of human territories and face-to-face interaction. He holds a M.Sc. in Computer Science from both Reykjavik University and Camerino University.
