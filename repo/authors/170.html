Veijo Pönni
  Veijo Pönni
M.Soc.Sc. (Econ.) Veijo Pönni, is a project researcher in BID – Business and Innovations Development. He has more than a decade of research expertise in business economics and statistics - including financial statements analysis of companies – applied into the field of media and cultural industries and also lately into tourism industry. His expertise focuses especially in the graphic arts, advertising, publishing and music industries as well as economics of tourism. His research work has been commissioned by industry organizations, government ministries and the European Commission. Parallel to his research work Mr. Pönni has also participated into several projects concerning various Business Development processes and lately he has been involved in managing of a Business Development laboratory. During the past decade he has also gathered experience in various teaching positions both in the Turku School of Economics and in the Turku University of Applied Sciences. His most recent research interests are focused in the business of leisure boating and travelling in the area of Finnish Archipelago.
