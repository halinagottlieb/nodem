Nao Saito
  Nao Saito
Designer and researcher based in Tokyo, Japan. Graduated from Architecture (BA) at Waseda University, Japan and Spatial and furniture design (MA) at University of Art and Design Helsinki, Finland. Design Furniture and object in architecture, considering that furniture is medium between people using their five senses in the space. Main works: Tactile map for Tokyo National Museum(2011, Japan), furniture and workshop design in architecture project of kindergarten (2012, NASCA ,Japan), ’Path’, installation and workshop, at Design museum and Alvar Aalto museum (2005/2006, Finland), Tactile map for Finnish Federation for Visually Impaired (2004, Finland).
