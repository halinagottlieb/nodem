Flemming Tvede Hansen
  Flemming Tvede Hansen
Flemming Tvede Hansen is a graduate student from the Danish Design School 1990-95 specialized in ceramics and glass and subsequently trained in multimedia design 1999-2000 at The Multimedia Institute MMI at The School of Architecture in Copenhagen. His Ph.D. Scholar was delivered and defended in 2010 at The Danish Design School, Copenhagen and is about how experiential knowledge of crafts rooted in ceramics can be transformed and utilized in the use of digital technologies. His current research is about how experiential knowledge and the involvement of the body are being exploited in the use of digital technology. It can be hand gestures, body movement, or the voice, that forms the basis for an interaction with a digital responding system. Flemming Tvede Hansen is currently working as a post-doctoral research fellow at the The Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts - The School of Design.
