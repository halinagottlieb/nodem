Juho Pääkylä
  Juho Pääkylä
B.Sc. (Tech.) Juho Pääkylä does research and software development on mixed reality technologies at the Technology Research Center at the University of Turku. He is experienced in creating two dimensional and three dimensional content. His work ranges from 3D modeling, texturing, graphical design to animation. His bachelor thesis he wrote about multicore programming environments.
