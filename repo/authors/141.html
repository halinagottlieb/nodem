Steven Sasseville
  Steven Sasseville
Steven is Chief Creative Officer and Managing Partner of Orange Hive, Frankfurt. He frequently speaks about design thinking, the power of making things and the hidden potentials of daydreaming to students, staff and customers. A classical painter by training, Steven is focused on managing and hopefully inspiring creativity. He likes working hands-on and has always been a separatist, fostering independence and rigor in order to achieve something new. He gives people a lot of room, space to think, but expects them to contribute: "Everyone has to step up and show what they are capable of". Steven also feels that creativity stems from culture and forming lasting relationships. Orange Hive is a Frankfurt based group of designers, coders and thinkers operating within the fields of design, communication, research and development. In his role as CCO at Orange Hive, he works with clients such as GE, Fiat, Pentahotels, Sony Mobile, Malik Management, Museum of Applied Arts, Frankfurt, Städel Museum and the Schader Foundation.
