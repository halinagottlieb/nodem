Jimin Rhim
  Jimin Rhim http://ct.kaist.ac.kr/main.php...
Jimin Rhim is in her doctoral program at the Graduate School of Culture Technology, KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), Dae-jeon, Korea. She has received her bachelor’s degree and master’s degree in Industrial Design from KAIST, Korea. She is currently a researcher in Serious Game Group and Product & Environment System Design Research Lab. Her research interests include human robot interaction, designing situated behaviors of social robots, serious game design, and developmental interaction for children.
