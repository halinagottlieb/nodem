Merel van der Vaart
  Merel van der Vaart http://www.uva.nl/over-de-uva/...
Merel van der Vaart is a PhD student at the University of Amsterdam, the Netherlands. She is based at the Allard Pierson Museum, the University’s archaeology museum. Her research focusses on the (changing) role of the physical museum object as part of ‘technology-heavy’ museum displays. So far, her research has explored the use of Augmented Reality and virtually restored object replicas. She previously worked at the Science Museum in London as its first Associate Curator of Public History, where she was responsible for co-creative projects both online and on-site. In 2010 she was Research Assistant for the LIVE!Museum project at the University of Leicester’s School of Museum Studies. She wrote her master thesis on the way on-site digital installations impact on social interaction among visitors. Special interests include: the relationship between people, objects and information; audience engagement, participation & co-creation; social interaction in the museum space; and merging online and on-site experiences. Merel holds degrees in Museology (cum laude) and Language & Culture Studies , as well as an MA in Museum Studies.
