Sami Jokela
  Sami Jokela
B.Sc. (Tech) Sami Jokela works as research assistant at the University of Turku, Technology Research Center (TRC). His major is software engineering and game development as a minor. He has experience in web- and mobile application development from various projects. Jokela is working on his master’s thesis “Accessibility of depth-sensor based applications and natural user interfaces”.
