Tara Baoth-Mooney
  Tara Baoth-Mooney
Artist in Residence, Researcher, SMARTlab Research Institute, University College Dublin, Ireland. Tara Baoth Mooney is an artist in residence and research fellow at SMARTlab University College Dublin. She holds a BA in Music and Theology from St. Patrick’s Pontifical University, Ireland. She also holds a Masters in Fashion and the Environment from the University of the Arts London. She has trained and worked as a designer in New York, London, Ireland, Bangladesh, India and China. Tara has also trained and worked as a performer and composer for children’s television in New York at the Jim Henson company. Recently, she has worked on the development of a series for Irish National Television. Tara sees technology as a tool or means to facilitate communication, provoking reflection as to how we interact with both our personal and immediate environments. In her work, she marries elements of technology with performance and craft and it is, she believes, within this area of crossover that interaction, empowerment and creative awareness have the potential to bring together the individual, the wider community and the environment within which they sit. Tara has exhibited her work at the Cite de la mode et du design, Paris, The Barbican, London, Victoria House, London, She was also selected to participate in the UNESCO World Heritage funded project ‘Hindustan’ as a working artist.
