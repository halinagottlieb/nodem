Åsa Lindgren
  Åsa Lindgren http://www.tekniskamuseet.se
Åsa Lindgren is the Project Manager of the new science center at the National Museum of Science and Technology in Stockholm; a 2,000 square-foot exhibition and learning laboratory. She has previously produced the museums travelling exhibition “The Inventions of Women” for Swedish and European audiences, in collaboration with the Swedish Institute, as well as managed the communication strategy for the world heritage Laponia and the public activities at the world heritage Falun Mine. She is a cultural scientist and communicator and has a broad experience of management, sponsorships and collaboration. In this project, the objective is for a science center where everyone can play, create and learn – together. Involving the target audience is a way of making sure that the result will be inclusive, accessible and dynamic.
