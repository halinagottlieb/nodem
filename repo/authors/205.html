Ana Isabel Ribeiro
  Ana Isabel Ribeiro www.m-almada.pt/casadacerca
Director of the ‘Casa da Cerca’ – Center of Comntemporary Art (Municipality of Almada) since 2002. She holds a BA in History from University of Lisbon – School of Literature (1982). She holds a Master in Contemporary Art History from Social Sciences and Humanities University (1994). She develops work in Arts, Public Art and Architecture research field. She is the author and co-author of more than a hundred texts and papers, published in specialized editions of public art and artistic domain. She regularly participates in international conferences and seminars disclosing the production, management and dissemination activities held by the Municipality of Almada in its public art heritage, which she is the responsible coordinator.
