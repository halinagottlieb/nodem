Aleksandra Janus
  Aleksandra Janus
Aleksandra Janus is a PhD candidate at the Jagiellonian University in Cracow, a researcher and activist. She is especially interested in the politics of heritage and in her research focuses on the ways in which museums and heritage institutions shape national and cultural identity. In particular, she tries to examine how these institutions deal with difficult heritage and how they challenge and affect public memory. She is involved in numerous initiatives promoting openness and participatory models of work in the GLAM sector. She was a speaker at i.a.: the MuseumNEXT conference (Barcelona 2012) and annual conferences of ICOM Comcol (Berlin 2011, Cape Town 2012).
