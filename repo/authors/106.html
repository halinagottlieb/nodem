Nuno Duarte Martins
  Nuno Duarte Martins
Researcher at ID+, Research Institute for Design, Media and Culture (www.idmais.org). Teacher at Polytechnic Institute of Cávado e Ave, in Barcelos, Portugal (www.est.ipca.pt). / Nuno Duarte Martins holds a doctorate in Digital Media at the Engineering Faculty of University of Porto, Portugal (2013) and holds a Master’s degree in Multimedia Art from the Fine-Arts School of The University of Porto (2007). He is a Designer and also teaches at Polytechnic Institute of Cávado e Ave, in Barcelos, Portugal. He is also a member of ID+, Research Institute on Design, Media and Culture.
