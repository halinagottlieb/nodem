Corresponding author Chiel van den Akker is a postdoctoral researcher in the Agora project and lecturer Historical Theory at the VU University Amsterdam. He received both his MA in History (cum laude) (1997) and his PhD in Philosophy (2009) from the Radboud University Nijmegen (The Netherlands). http://www.let.vu.nl/nl/organisatie-van-de-faculteit/wetenschappelijk-personeel/medewerkers-alfabetisch/medewerkers-a-d/c-van-den-akker/index.asp Agora project Website: http://agora.cs.vu.nl/
