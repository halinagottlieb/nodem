Maria Roussou
  Maria Roussou http://www.madgik.di.uoa.gr/pe...
Dr Maria Roussou is an interaction designer and researcher, engaged in combining innovative technology with education and culture. For CHESS, Maria manages the User-Centred Design work. Besides CHESS, Maria has been teaching HCI as well as the Museums and New Technologies course of the Museum Studies graduate program at the University of Athens. She is also a founding director of makebelieve design & consulting (www.makebelieve.gr). Previously, she had established (in 1998) and directed (1998-2003) the Virtual Reality Department at the Foundation of the Hellenic World in Athens. She has also collaborated with many other museums including the Walker Art Center in Minneapolis, USA. Maria holds a PhD in Computer Science (VR/HCI) from the University of London (University College London), a Master in Fine Arts (MFA) in Electronic Visualization and a MSc in Computer Science from the University of Illinois at Chicago, and a BSc from the Athens University of Economics and Business.
