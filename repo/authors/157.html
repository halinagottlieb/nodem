Alessandra Spagnoli
  Alessandra Spagnoli http://www.dipartimentodesign....
Interior e product designer. Lecturer at Politecnico di Milano, Design School. Grant researcher in 2013 funded by Fondazione Fratelli Confalonieri with a research titled “Memoria del progetto & sistemi temporanei di fruizione culturale”. PhD in Design and Technologies for Cultural Heritage enhancement in 2011. Her research interests focus on interior design, temporary events, communication and new models for cultural heritage fruition.
  • Design & new models of archiving, documenting, visualizing and enhancing the design culture
  • NODEM 2013 - E. Lupo - Concluding Remarks - "Digital Archive of Cultural Temporary Exhibitions"
