Chantal Eschenfelder
  Chantal Eschenfelder http://www.staedelmuseum.de
Chantal Eschenfelder is Head of Education of the Städel Museum and Liebighaus Skulpturensammlung and the Schirn Kunsthalle in Frankfurt/Main, Germany. Born 1965, she studied History of Art, German Literature and American Civilization in Paris and Munich where she earned her Ph.D. She has published books on French and Italian art in times of manierism as well as educational books for children.
