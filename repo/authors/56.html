Nicholas Foulcher
  Nicholas Foulcher
Phd Candidate of Architecture, School of Architecture and Built Environment, Newcastle, Australia. Nicholas Foulcher is a Casual Academic and Research Assistant at the School of Architecture and Built Environment at the University of Newcastle, Australia. He holds a Bachelor of Design (Arch), a Master of Architecture (Hons1) and is currently a PhD candidate (Arch) at the University of Newcastle. His current projects include studies into technologies impact on architectural education, and the reconstruction and preservation of architectural heritage using Augmented Reality.
