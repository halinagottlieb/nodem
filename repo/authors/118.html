John Coburn
  John Coburn http://www.twmuseums.org.uk
John Coburn is a Digital Coordinator at Tyne & Wear Archives & Museums managing digital projects that experiment with the role and public value of museum collections. John has worked in the cultural heritage sector for over 10 years on a range of innovative projects that have engaged audiences of millions and inspired communities from the wider arts and technology world to access and reuse collections. John established and co-produced the successful Culture Code initiative, coordinated the Culture Shock! digital storytelling project and developed the acclaimed Hidden Newcastle app. He also chairs his region’s Digital Culture group and is a member of British Museum’s Fresh Leads leadership programme.
