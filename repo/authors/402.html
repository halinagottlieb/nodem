Elvan Özkavruk Adanır
  Elvan Özkavruk Adanır
ELVAN OZKAVRUK ADANIR, is a studied textile engineering at Ege University. She got her master and doctorate’s degree in art at Dokuz Eylül University, respectively in 1992 and 1997. She worked as research assistant, lecturer, assistant professor and associate professor of traditional arts at Dokuz Eylül University between 1990 and 2008. In 2008 she got her professorship. She published four books, 22 papers, 8 articles, and 2 book chapters. She translated the book written by J. Bronowski-B. Mazlish named “Western Intellectual Tradition from Leonardo to Hegel”. She opened and attended solo and mixed exhibitions. Her art works are accepted for international and national permanent collections.
