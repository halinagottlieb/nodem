Raquel Barros Pinto
  Raquel Barros Pinto https://www.linkedin.com/profi...
She is a PhD student in Communication Sciences since 2012, at the University of Minho, in the area of specialization Interactive Media. She receives the Master degree in Teaching Visual Arts in the 3rd Cycle of Basic Education and Secondary Education in 2012, at the Faculty of Psychology and Educational Sciences of the University of Porto, with the thesis entitled "Daily chart as a learning device design in secondary education". In 2010 she obtained the Master degree in in Digital Art and Technology, at University of Minho, with the thesis entitled "Game of Checkers, one Recreation Grounded in Interactive Storytelling". In 2008 she obtained the BsC degree in Art / Design, at the Arts School of Porto, and a Bachelor degree in Design, at the Superior Artistic School of the Port, in 1999.
