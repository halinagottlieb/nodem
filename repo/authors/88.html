Christian Hviid Mortensen
  Christian Hviid Mortensen http://www.dream.dk
Christian Hviid Mortensen is associated researcher with DREAM. Currently he is a PhD Fellow at the Department for the study of culture, University of Southern Denmark and Curator at the Media Museum in Odense, Denmark (since 2007). He is doing his thesis on Radio as intangible heritage in a museological context. His primary research interest is in media heritage and popular culture which sit at the intersection of museology with media, cultural, memory and heritage studies.
