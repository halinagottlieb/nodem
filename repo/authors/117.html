Caroline Claisse
  Caroline Claisse http://rcaied.tumblr.com/
Caroline Claisse is an artist, designer and researcher specialising in museums, meaning making and creativity. She just graduated from the MA Information Experience Design (IED) at the Royal College of Art and is currently an IED visiting lecturer. Recently she has designed materials for Historic Royal Palaces, English Heritage and the National Trust, as well as exhibiting her own work in UK and France.
