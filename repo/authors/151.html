Li Jönsson
  Li Jönsson
Li Jönsson is currently doing a PhD in interaction design at The Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts - The School of Design. The PhD-project is set in the context of new technology & innovations project, collaborating across a various of disciplines, engaging a multitude of different actors. Within this constellation her interest is to explore how a design practice moves from statements and signification, too a design that has to do with a doing and becoming through the questions: What does a design event spark into being? How can design intervene and allow new kinds of actors, or different publics to emerge? She has previously worked with design research at Interactive Institute in Sweden, and has also previously engaged in design curation for the London Design Week.
