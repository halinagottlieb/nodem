Minseok Kim
  Minseok Kim http://ct.kaist.ac.kr/main.php...
Minseok Kim is a master's student at the Graduate School of Culture Technology, KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), Dae-jeon, Korea. He received his B.E. in Cultural Contents and B.B.A. in Business Administration from the Catholic University of Korea, Seoul and has internship experience at Samjong KPMG and the Korea Creative Contents Agency. Currently, He is working in The Cultural Management & Policy Lab (CMPL), directed by Professor Wonjoon Kim at KAIST. Minseok Kim's field of study is business analysis and marketing strategy for cultural business and creative industry, with a particular focus on digital environments which involve technology innovation. His personal research interests include cultural planning & management, quantitative data analysis for data-driven marketing, social network analysis and cultural heritage in the digital age. Presently, he is working on a research paper about a serious game for cultural heritage preservation called ‘The Guardians’.
