Hyejeong Yeom
  Hyejeong Yeom http://ct.kaist.ac.kr/main.php...
Hyejeong Yeom is a master candidate in the Graduate School of Culture Technology at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology in Experience Lab. She has received her bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, KAIST. She has worked in Building Facility Engineer, in Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Center, Samsung Construction & Trading and Building Facility Department, in Samsung Electrical Research Complex Construction Sites, Suwon, Korea.
