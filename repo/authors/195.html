Jungwha Kim
  Jungwha Kim http://ct.kaist.ac.kr/main.php...
Professor Jungwha Kim received her D.E.A. in Modern Plastic Art Theory from the University Paris Ⅳ Sorbonne and in French Literature from the University ParisⅢ Nouvelle-Sorbonne. She has 20 years experience in planning museum and museum exhibition. Currently, she is professor at the Graduate School of Culture Technology, KAIST and leading a research LAB on management and policy issues of museums and cultural institutions in relation with contemporary digital technology driven society.
