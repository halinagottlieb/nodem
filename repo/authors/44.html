Klu rgyal ‘bum Lijiaben
  Klu rgyal ‘bum Lijiaben
Plateau Culture Heritage Protection Group, Xining City, Oinghai Province, China. Klu rgyal ‘bum (Lijiaben) is a Tibetan from Qinghai Province. He graduated from Qinghai Normal University’s BA program with a dual major in English and Tibetan in 2011. Since graduation, Klurgyal ‘bum has worked in cultural preservation, helping to document the unique endangered cultural heritage of the Tibetan Plateau. As an archivist working for the World Oral Literature Project (Cambridge University) he prepared more than 400 songs and several dozen videos for online archiving, by preparing relevant metadata in English, Tibetan, and Chinese. He now works for the Plateau Cultural Heritage Protection Group, providing training on cultural awareness and to help young people acquire the technical skills necessary to document their communities’ traditions. He hopes that rural people across the Tibetan Plateau will continue to increase their awareness of the importance of cultural preservation, and will also increasingly have opportunities to act on this awareness.
