Linda Lotina
  Linda Lotina http://www.va.lv/
Linda Lotina is a PhD student in the Institute of Journalism, Communication and Information Studies, Faculty of Social Sciences and Education, University of Tartu, and works as a lecturer at the Vidzeme University of Applied Sciences, Faculty of Tourism and Hospitality Management. Her PhD thesis focuses on participation issues in (Latvian) museums in online and offline enviroments from pespective of museum`s visitors and proffesionals. Other research interests are related with tourist motivation studies and use of (Latvian) sacral heritage in tourism.
