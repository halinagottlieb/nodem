Lars Ulrich Tarp Hansen
  Lars Ulrich Tarp Hansen http://www.kunsten.dk
Lars Ulrich Tarp Hansen, Master of Arts, is Head of Communication at KUNSTEN Museum of Modern Art Aalborg since 2005. Besides being responsible for Public Relations and marketing, he is responsible for developing and implementing the digital communication – content as well as technical issues. Lars Ulrich is living the phrase “Think Big, start small, move fast” and, in every project, aims to reduce bureaucracy, streamline development costs and secure sustainable operations to ensure continuation when projects funds run out. Lars Ulrich has been the initiator and primary concept developer on the iGuide-project in which a number of Danish museums cooperated on the development of smartphone apps, based on DR’s (Danish Broadcasting Corporation) platform, CHAOS. The project has given small museums, which used to be unable to participate in digital projects, an opportunity to catch up with technology and achieve a sustainable platform, which forms a basis for future projects. Furthermore, Lars Ulrich has been advocating a journalistic approach in the production of content. This approach creates a large degree of presence in the relationship between curator and audience and is, furthermore, cost effective. The iGuide-project has given KUNSTEN huge savings in the operating expenses for IT as well as an increased efficiency in the workflow.
