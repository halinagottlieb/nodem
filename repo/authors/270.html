Slavko Milekic
  Slavko Milekic http://www.uarts.edu/users/smi...
Slavko Milekic holds a medical degree and MSci in Neuropsychology (Belgrade School of Medicine), as well as Ph.D. in Cognitive Science (University of Connecticut). His research interests include digital design, the psychology of human/computer interaction and building of WWW-based tools for knowledge transfer. Dr Milekic's innovative, child-friendly digital environment (KiddyFace) for art exploration has been implemented in a number of museums. He has been engaged as a consultant for the development of interactive digital environments by a number of institutions including the Philadelphia Museum of Art, National Constitution Center in Philadelphia, Phoenix Art Museum, Cleveland Museum of Art, and Interactive Institute in Stockholm, Sweden. Dr. Milekic's work has been recently featured in a PBS series exploring early childhood creativity, "Art to Heart."
