Lars Lischke
  Lars Lischke http://www.vis.uni-stuttgart.d...
Lars Lischke is a PhD student at Institute for Visualization and Interactive Systems, University of Stuttgart. In his PhD he is focusing on the interaction with large high resolution displays. He studied computer science at the University of Stuttgart. He has written his diploma thesis at the Institute for Visualization and Interactive Systems about digital interaction in museums. During his studies Lars focused on visualization, human computer interaction and on concepts of programming languages. He wrote his first project thesis at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden, in the field of mobile computing. As a student assistant Lars worked on different research projects mainly related to transport and computer science at the University of Stuttgart and for SSB AG, the principal public transport operating company in Stuttgart.
