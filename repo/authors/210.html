Karolina Ziulkoski
  Karolina Ziulkoski http://www.karolinaziulkoski.c...
Karolina Ziulkoski is an interactive exhibition designer from Brazil. She holds a Master of Professional Studies degree in Interactive Telecommunications from New York University, and bachelor’s degrees in Architecture from the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS – Brazil – summa cum laude) and Advertising from the Pontifical University of Rio Grande do Sul (PUCRS – Brazil). Her body of work ranges from installations to applications, always focusing on delivering content through meaningful digital experiences. After spending two years in New York City, one of them interning at the American Museum of Natural History at the Exhibition Design – Interactives & Media department, she is based in southern Brazil, where she is partner at Bolota, a company focused in interactive exhibition design.
