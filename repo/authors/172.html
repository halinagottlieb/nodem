Chris McConville
  Chris McConville http://www.vu.edu.au/‎
Dr Chris McConville is a Lecturer in Sociology Victoria University Melbourne Australia. He has worked on a number of projects in regards to the memory of Melbourne’s waterfront and has published widely in areas of history, heritage and public landscapes.
