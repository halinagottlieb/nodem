Mohammad Nabil
  Mohammad Nabil
I work at CULTNAT as the Head of the Research & Development. CULTNAT (The Centre for Documentation of Cultural and Natural Heritage), is one of Bibliotheca Alexandrina research centers stationed in Cairo that aims at applying the latest technological innovations to document Egypt’s Cultural and Natural Heritage. My main research interests are in the applications of advanced technologies in the field of 3D digitization and visualization of movable and immovable heritage.
