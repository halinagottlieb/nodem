Daniel Pletinckx
  Daniel Pletinckx http://www.visualdimension.be
After working for 14 years in industry in the domain of image processing and quality management, Daniel Pletinckx became a consultant in the application of ICT in cultural heritage in 1997. Since then, he is a chief consultant in the Ename 974 project, a major heritage presentation project in the village of Ename, Belgium, where he designed the first on-site virtual reality system that provides public interpretation of archaeological remains. Together with Dirk Callebaut, Neil Silberman and Jean-Pierre van der Meiren, he founded in 2000 the Ename Center for Public Archaeology and Heritage Presentation, that became an international knowledge centre in cultural heritage, where he designed several other interpretation systems and coordinated a variety of international projects. In 2004, he became Coordinator of Integration Activities in EPOCH, the European 6FP IST Network of Excellence for optimising the use of ICT in tangible cultural heritage. Since 2003, he has his own company Visual Dimension that provides consulting for the use of ICT in cultural heritage and tourism.
