Marc Tamschick
  Marc Tamschick http://tamschick.com/en
CEO, Creative Direction, Direction Marc Tamschick is the founder and creative lead of TMS. He is personally involved in all projects and creatively directs all of them. As CEO of the company, he manages the financial and legal decisions. Studied in Graphic Design at the Academy of fine Arts in Stuttgart, Dipl. Film Director of the Academy of Film Baden-Württemberg in Ludwigsburg (Diploma 1994). 1994-2002 Shareholder, Founder and General Manager of the Mediamutant Filmproduction GmbH & CO. KG. Producer, Creative Director and Director of numerous Music Clips, Commercials and Media Installations. Since 2002 Shareholder, Founder and General Manager of the Studio TAMSCHICK MEDIA+SPACE, Scenography and Filmproduction Company, Berlin (GmbH since 2007). Works as Media artist, Director and Producer for Scenographers, Agencies, Production Companies and clients worldwide. Works awarded at (Selection): ADC Deutschland, ADC New York, ADC Global, WorldMediaFestival, Best architects, Animago Award, New York Festivals, Digital Content Award, Prix Leonardo, Finalist Clip Attack, Videokunstpreis des Goethe Instituts, Intermedia Globe Gold. Teaching contracts at different Universities, lately in the masters programme in staging and scenic space at the Technical University in Berlin.
