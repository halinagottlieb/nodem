Magdalena Tafvelin Heldner
  Magdalena Tafvelin Heldner http://www.tekniskamuseet.se
Magdalena Tafvelin Heldner, curator at National Museum of Science and Technology and project manager of the exhibition 100 innovations - the museum largest exhibition so far. In a year she has taken care of making this large and complex exhibition together with her team. She has worked at the museum since 1998 and has handled exhibitions and documentations in various and very different areas of the industrial heritage - from vacuum cleaners to nuclear power plants.
