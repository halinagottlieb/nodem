Robert Gillesse
  Robert Gillesse
Robert Gillesse (1967) studied history at the University of Leiden. He has worked as researcher and quality manager for different digitization projects at the National Library of the Netherlands (KB) and the City Archives of Amsterdam. Currently he works as senior consultant at the DEN (Digital Heritage Netherlands) Foundation. In this capacity he is responsible for the stimulation of standardization and interoperability of digitization projects within the cultural heritage sector. He specializes in digital strategy, digital preservation, image quality assurance and still image file formats.
