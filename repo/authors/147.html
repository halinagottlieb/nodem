Bianca Bocatius
  Bianca Bocatius http://www.phil-fak.uni-duesse...
Since 2010 Bianca Bocatius has been a PhD candidate at the Department of Information Science at the University Düsseldorf, Germany. She investigates the field of museum education with the use of social media in German museums. With her thesis Ms. Bocatius combines the topics of museum education, knowledge management with ICT, pedagogical theories and socio-technological developments to elaborate on the possibilities and challenges with social media for museum education.
