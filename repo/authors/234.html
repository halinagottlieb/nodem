Karin von Ompteda
  Karin von Ompteda http://karinvonompteda.com/
Karin von Ompteda is a biologist turned graphic designer turned doctoral researcher at the Royal College of Art in London. Her work uses data visualisation to explore design artifacts, bridge scientific and design knowledge, and stimulate new thinking about creative practice. Karin’s visualisation work has been published, presented and exhibited internationally; highlights include Grafik Magazine, TYPO London and BIO.23 Biennial of Design, respectively. Karin is a Visiting Lecturer in the School of Communication’s newest MA course – Information Experience Design.
