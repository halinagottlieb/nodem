Karsten Gresch
  Karsten Gresch http://www.softwareag.com
Karsten Gresch (1968) is a lawyer and computer engineer. He earned his assessor iuris (law master equivalent) at the Freie Universität Berlin, worked as an advocate and finally switched to the IT business where he worked for different companies (amongst them Skandia) in various technical positions. At his current employer, Software AG, he is the technical responsible for the cloud media platform, a research project offering a semantic search engine for art exhibits, books and more (http://is.gd/cloudmediasag). Karsten also founded the Lean Museum Startup initiative (http://leanmusemstartup.com) and has special interests in entrepreneurship, business model generation and all facets of innovation and interdisciplinarity. Thanks to becoming well-bred through family and education, he loves the arts, e.g. Venetian painting from Renaissance to Tiepolo, contemporary arts, poetry, classical music (both historic and contemporary) and the many interfaces between arts and IT. Karsten is married and father of five (2+3) children.
