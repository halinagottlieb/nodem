Nina Udby Granlie
  Nina Udby Granlie www.trapholt.dk
Nina Granlie is Collection Manager at art and design museum Trapholt in Denmark. She has a master in Art history, Museology and Webcommunication and has worked with both art and digital media in several art projects. She has a background working in interaction design agencies and as a coordinator of a Cultural Center.
