Marko Raat
  Marko Raat
Marko Raat is a film director, working both in the area of fiction film and documentaries. He has made documentaries, shorts, TV productions, two full-length feature films (Agent Wild Duck, 2002, Knife, 2007), directed in theatre and has been involved in several art projects. Since 2009 he is part of the exhibition designers team working with the Estonian National Museums upcoming permanent exhibitions as well as numerous side projects related to that. He is responsible for AV-design concept of the exhibitions as well as creating film- and videowork items.
