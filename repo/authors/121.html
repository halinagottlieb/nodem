Erik Kristiansen
  Erik Kristiansen
Erik Kristiansen holds an MA in general linguistics and computer science from the University of Copenhagen and received his PhD in performance design from Roskilde University, Denmark. He is an assistant professor of performance design at Roskilde University and conducts research within the performance of play, pervasive games, creativity and interactive museum installations. He is currently working on a contribution on design for an anthology on the Danish designer and multi talent, Piet Hein. Recent publications include ‘Design games for in-situ design’, International Journal of Mobile Human Computer Interaction 5, 3 (2013), The transformative museum: Conference proceedings (editor, DREAM 2012, http://www.dream.dk/?q=en/communication/transformative-museum-proceedings), 'The museum lobby as a transformative space', conference paper, DREAM international conference The Transformative Museum, Roskilde, Denmark, 23-25 May 2012 (co-author, see: http://www.dream.dk/?q=en/communication/transformative-museum-proceedings).
