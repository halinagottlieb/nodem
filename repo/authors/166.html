Juha-Pekka Arimaa
  Juha-Pekka Arimaa
M.Sc. (Tech.) Juha-Pekka Arimaa is a project researcher at the University of Turku, Business and Innovation Development (BID) Unit. His educational background considers software engineering, business economics and hypermedia. He has several years’ experience in software development and project work and currently he acts as responsible of technical matters in a research project. Arimaa has experience in both back and front-end development with multiple different programming languages.
