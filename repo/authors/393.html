Giovanni Verreschi
  Giovanni Verreschi www.ettsolutions.com
Giovanni Verreschi is CEO and President of ETT S.p.A. He graduated in Electronic Engineering in 1993, then he received a Ph.D. in Electronic and Computer Engineering, focusing his research on neuro-engineering.rnIn 1996 he started his entrepreneurial career founding Studio P&V.rnIn 2000, as founding member and CEO, created ETT, an ICT and consulting company, which operates in the following main business areas: New-media Solutions (Cultural heritage and Nature sites, Museum System, Tourism, Education, Corporate marketing), Research and Development (Innovative devices and technologies, Neuro-engineering, e-Health, Environmental Data Observation Systems), Smart Government (Web Solutions for Public Administration, Job Centres), ICT Consulting Services (Smart Government, Clinical Engineering, Finance).
