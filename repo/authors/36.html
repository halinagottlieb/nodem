Ryan Francis Reyes
  Ryan Francis Reyes
Lecturer, San Beda College, Manila, Philippines. MA Candidate, Southeast Asian Studies, University of the Philippines, Vargas Museum. Ryan Francis Reyes has been in the museum profession for over four years, working as a museum assistant at the main museum of the University of the Philippines, where he also graduated with a degree in Art Studies. He assists in key areas of the museum programming such as education, exhibition planning, and curatorial aspect. Now pursuing his master’s degree in Southeast Asian Studies in the same university, he is currently doing research on the intimations of nationalism and identity among ethnographic museums in northern Thailand dedicated to the hill tribe peoples. He is also a lecturer at San Beda College in Manila.
